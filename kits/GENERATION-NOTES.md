# Source-generation record

All expansion sheets were generated using the built-in image-generation tool, with the recovered original Cattle Trail sheets/style images as visual references. They are new derivatives, not falsely labeled original conversation assets. Raw originals remain in ../source with their own provenance.

generation-jobs.json records saved base prompts and per-sheet briefs. It is a generation recipe, not a byte-for-byte transcript of every tool request: some calls appended stronger facing/spacing reminders; grass and trees were generated before the batched job catalog. Their preserved source PNGs and manifest checksums identify the exact delivered pixels. Grass requested sixteen coherent Texas prairie ground-cover variations; trees requested sixteen coherent warm high-three-quarter pixel-art tree silhouettes. Both used original reference_2.png and isolated magenta backgrounds.

Rustler west-v2 and north-v2 used the generated rustler-east sheet as an additional direct identity reference after the original-reference attempts faced the wrong way. Replaced sheets remain preserved and excluded from the manifest count. The dense rider-v1 pilot remains rejected for unusable layout and background.

Extraction is deterministic from the saved PNGs. Regenerating images from the same recipe is not expected to recreate identical pixels.
