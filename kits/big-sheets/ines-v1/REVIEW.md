This is four actual generation outputs, not repeated exports or duplicate padding. Exact prompts, raw images and source hashes are preserved. The requested resolution was3840px wide; actual outputs are1402×1122 for sheet1,1724×912 for sheet2, and1723×913 for sheets3/4.

Sheet1 contains SE/NE/E walking candidates, static directions, spirit actions and social poses. Sheet2 adds NW/N/NE/W walk candidates. Sheet3 adds E/SE/S/SW running candidates. Sheet4 adds idle breathing, spirit-tracking, lantern-carry walking and conversation gestures. Observed silhouettes do change, especially running/actions, but several walk rows repeat their leading-leg configuration. Requested eight-phase labels therefore remain unverified; no complete loop is declared from the requested row names. The idle row includes very similar poses. The direction row in sheet1 is not a reliable eight-angle set.

All four requested transparency but returned RGB with a baked checkerboard. Raw files remain unchanged. Boundary-connected bright-neutral keying produced transparent derivatives, with native grid cells and figure crops retained. Keyed sheets were inspected. There are white edge remnants, enclosed checker fragments and tight gutter/cut warnings; these derivatives are candidate extraction work, not ready-to-ship alpha. Sheet4 in particular places figures very close to row boundaries and some lowest footwear reaches the canvas edge. Warning flags are recorded per cell, not hidden. No body parts were painted or reconstructed.

Atlas cells are256×256 with [128,244] anchors, but figures retain actual native pixel sizes without per-frame scaling. Atlas centering is only a production preview and does not establish stable anatomical animation registration. Native sources retain the evidence needed to correct registration and phase order. No additional pixels were generated by upscaling a small sprite. No candidate art is admitted to runtime and animation dictionaries remain empty until the actual sequence is established.
