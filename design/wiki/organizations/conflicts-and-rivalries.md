# Conflicts and rivalries

A map of who's actually opposed to whom, and on what terms — separated by stakes, since a water dispute, a trade rivalry, and an actual bad actor aren't the same shape of conflict and shouldn't read as one.

## Land and water (the big one)

The [Calloway Spread](calloway-spread.md) and [Hargrove Range](hargrove-range.md) are rivals for water, grass, and market timing — not enemies. Both outfits commissioned surveys of the same disputed stretch without realizing they'd conflict: **[Beatrix Lowe](../people/beatrix_lowe.md)**'s formal instrument survey against **[Matilda Greaves](../people/matilda_greaves.md)**'s intuitive, trail-experience one. **[County Land Office Clerk Purdy](../people/the_greaves_survey_client.md)** commissioned both by accident, hedging between a surveyor who costs more and one who's faster. Their resolution ("The Two Maps That Disagreed") is written to end in the two surveyors reconciling their methods, not one proving the other wrong — neither rival ranch "wins" the water outright by out-surveying the other.

## Trade rivalries (nobody's actually a villain)

- **[Naomi Freeman](../people/naomi_freeman.md)** vs. **[Orin Teague](../people/orin_teague.md)** — a jealous rival blacksmith who sends riders past her shop to check if she's failed yet. His wife **[Marta Teague](../people/the_hollis_teague_mother.md)** wants the grudge to end, and his own apprentice **[Hollis](../people/hollis_teague.md)** privately admires Naomi's work more than his master's.
- **[Marisol Vasquez](../people/marisol_vasquez.md)** vs. an unnamed cheaper rope-maker undercutting her at **[Cyrus Boone](../people/the_hemp_works_owner.md)**'s hemp works — resolved by her proving her rope's quality under real load, not by driving the rival out.
- **[Wilhelmina Frost](../people/wilhelmina_frost.md)** vs. **[Cato Villanueva](../people/the_frost_apprentice_rival.md)** — a purely competitive, non-dangerous rivalry; he undercuts her on price, she keeps winning the good commissions on quality.
- **[Prisca Montaigne](../people/prisca_montaigne.md)** vs. **[Silas Cobbett](../people/the_montaigne_rival_editor.md)** at [Providence](providence.md) — truth against what sells, with Cobbett admitting he's "a little embarrassed, occasionally" rather than sorry.

## Genuine antagonists (resolved by evidence or pressure, not villainy for its own sake)

Written deliberately non-violent or non-supernatural wherever possible, per this world's preference for ordinary human friction over melodrama:

- **[The Widow Stroud](../people/the_widow_stroud.md)** — financial pressure, resolvable by paying a debt, not fighting anyone. Her clerk **[Mortimer Vance](../people/the_stroud_clerk.md)** is visibly uncomfortable doing the collecting.
- **[Inspector Wexler](../people/the_kraus_tax_official.md)** — bureaucratic pressure on **[Adelheid Kraus](../people/adelheid_kraus.md)**'s distillery, resolvable by correct paperwork.
- **[Elias Thorndike](../people/the_mine_owner.md)** — a mine owner cutting corners on safety, resolvable by proving the hazard publicly, not by force, per **[Birgitta Holm](../people/birgitta_holm.md)**'s adventure.
- **[Reeve Talmadge](../people/the_finsbury_skeptic.md)** — reputational, not physical: public ridicule threatening **[Electra Finsbury](../people/electra_finsbury.md)**'s credibility and funding.
- **[Dr. Barnabus Thistlewood](../people/the_okonkwo_quack.md)** — a patent-medicine huckster defeated by **[Dinah Okonkwo](../people/dinah_okonkwo.md)**'s evidence, not a confrontation.
- **[Cutter Boyle](../people/cutter_boyle.md)** — the county's one deliberately negotiable hired gun: a professional, not a fanatic, who has walked off three jobs this year already.
- **[Amos Ketch](../people/the_hendricks_rival_crew_boss.md)** (see [Independent trail crews](independent-outfits.md)) — the clearest actual bad actor in the county, specifically so the two big rival ranches don't have to carry that weight themselves.

## Personal, small-scale friction

- **[Hosea Pruett](../people/the_wexford_dairy_neighbor.md)** vs. **[Briony Wexford](../people/briony_wexford.md)** — an ordinary neighbor dispute over water for her dairy herd, deliberately scaled down from the county's bigger land conflicts.
- **[Gustaf Renner](../people/the_ferry_rival.md)** vs. **[Clementina Reyes-Boone](../people/clementina_reyesboone.md)** — reckless competition risking lives to save an hour, resolved by her reading the water correctly when it matters.
- **[Chester Vane](../people/the_holt_auction_rival.md)** — a corrupt rival auctioneer **[Birdena Holt](../people/birdena_holt.md)** out-performs rather than exposes.
- **[Victor Ashgrove](../people/the_troupe_manager.md)** — the theater manager who actually absconded with **[Marlowe Castellano](../people/marlowe_castellano.md)**'s troupe's money; a thief, not a threat.
- **[Fenwick Gray](../people/the_dunmore_old_associate.md)** — the specific, ongoing, sincerely-meant pressure to pull **[Roisin Dunmore](../people/roisin_dunmore.md)** back into her old trade; harder to refuse than a threat because he isn't one.
