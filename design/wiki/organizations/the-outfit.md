# The Outfit

*The player's own household. See [GAME-DESIGN.md](../../../GAME-DESIGN.md) for the mechanics of recruitment and camp life, and [LORE.md](../../LORE.md) for what "outfit" means in this world's terms.*

## Who's on it

**The trail boss** — the player. No secret bloodline, no branded destiny. A horse, a rope, and a small herd he's trying to keep together.

**Recruited companions**, in the order they can join:

| Person | Role | Recruited via |
|---|---|---|
| [Eleanor](../people/eleanor.md) | Healer | Gathering the herd and clearing the rustler |
| [Ada Mercer](../people/ada_mercer.md) | Steam mechanic | Repairing her stalled walker |
| [Ines Vale](../people/ines_vale.md) | Spirit-listener and scout | Completing the three-landmark spirit trail (supersedes an earlier "Rosa Vale" draft of the same role — see [ines_vale.md](../people/ines_vale.md)) |
| [Birdie Calloway](../people/birdie_calloway.md) | Camp cook and singer | Talking with her, hearing her sing, then inviting her — no puzzle, gated behind Ines's recruitment |

Every other companion in [characters.json](../../characters.json) (67 more as of this writing) is written and ready but not yet placed in a room; see each one's own page for status.

**Standing crew**, present before the story starts and not recruitable — they don't leave, they don't get a relationship arc, they're just who already rides for this outfit:

| Person | Role |
|---|---|
| [Pardo](../people/pardo.md) | Senior hand, taught the player the actual work years back |
| [Alma Okafor](../people/point_rider_alma.md) | Point rider, reads the trail ahead of the herd |
| ["Young" Dutch](../people/young_dutch.md) | Newest hand, still enthusiastic |

The rest of the crew, also standing and not yet given a table row of their own:

- [Maren "Cookie" Holt](../people/outfit_cook_maren.md), chuckwagon cook, keeps the fire and the outfit's unofficial ledger of who's owed what favor.
- [Jonas Whitfield](../people/remuda_wrangler_jonas.md), horse wrangler, manages the remuda separately from the cattle herd — the same ground where the outfit would first meet the still-unplaced [Sable Quinn](../people/sable_quinn.md), a remuda wrangler of her own hunting for a fairer outfit to ride for.
- [Coy Ashworth](../people/night_herder_coy.md), night herder, treats a bad night the way [Ines Vale](../people/ines_vale.md) treats a bad track — information, not omen — and is usually the first hand to notice something like the [Night Birds](../people/the_night_birds.md) that leave no tracks.
- [Del Ochoa](../people/outfit_farrier_del.md), farrier, the ordinary-mechanical counterpart to [Ada Mercer](../people/ada_mercer.md)'s steam work — the hand to loop in when her contraptions need an anvil instead of a clever fix.
- [Pim Sorensen](../people/drag_rider_pim.md), rides drag, and wants nothing more than [Pardo](../people/pardo.md)'s word that he's ready to ride point.

## How it actually works

Recruitment, relationship, and camp assignment are tracked separately per [GAME-DESIGN.md](../../../GAME-DESIGN.md)'s state contracts — a companion can be in the household and stay unpartnered, and being hired is never a purchase of consent. Camp is where the ledger gets honest about who's strained and who can ride, per [LORE.md](../../LORE.md)'s household section.

## Who the outfit is up against

The two established rival ranches — [the Calloway Spread](calloway-spread.md) and [the Hargrove Range](hargrove-range.md) — compete for the same water and grass but aren't villains by default. [Silas Rourke](../people/silas_rourke.md), the rustler already in the room, wants the same market and has his own strain. See [conflicts-and-rivalries.md](conflicts-and-rivalries.md) for the full map of who's actually opposed to whom in this county.
