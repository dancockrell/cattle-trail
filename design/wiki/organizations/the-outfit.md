## How it actually works

Recruitment, relationship, and camp assignment are tracked separately per [GAME-DESIGN.md](../../../GAME-DESIGN.md)'s state contracts — a companion can be in the household and stay unpartnered, and being hired is never a purchase of consent. Camp is where the ledger gets honest about who's strained and who can ride, per [LORE.md](../../LORE.md)'s household section.

## Who the outfit is up against

The two established rival ranches — [the Calloway Spread](calloway-spread.md) and [the Hargrove Range](hargrove-range.md) — compete for the same water and grass but aren't villains by default. [Silas Rourke](../people/silas_rourke.md), the rustler already in the room, wants the same market and has his own strain. See [conflicts-and-rivalries.md](conflicts-and-rivalries.md) for the full map of who's actually opposed to whom in this county.
