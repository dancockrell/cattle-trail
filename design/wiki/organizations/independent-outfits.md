# Independent trail crews

Not every outfit in the county is a big established ranch. Two small independent operations, deliberately written as a contrasting pair:

## Zilpah Hendricks's crew

**[Zilpah Hendricks](../people/zilpah_hendricks.md)** runs a small, established, and genuinely fair independent crew — commanding without being harsh, protective of her people, respected by those who work for her. She's a deliberate older parallel to **[Dorothea Farrow](../people/dorothea_farrow.md)**, not a rival: a possible mentor if that thread is ever picked up.

## Amos Ketch's crew

**[Amos Ketch](../people/the_hendricks_rival_crew_boss.md)** runs the county's clearest non-supernatural bad actor — not a rival ranch owner with understandable motives like the [Calloways](calloway-spread.md) or [Hargroves](hargrove-range.md), but an operator who actually treats his own crew badly and is only friendly to outsiders when they're useful to him. He exists specifically so the county's two big rival ranches can stay morally mixed rather than needing to absorb all of its villainy themselves.
