# The Calloway Spread

*See [LOCATIONS.md](../../LOCATIONS.md#the-calloway-spread) for the place itself.*

A neighboring cattle operation, larger and better-fenced than the player's outfit, run by a family that's been in this county longer. Not enemies by default — rivals for water, grass, and market timing, which is friction enough without inventing villainy.

## The family

- **[Cole Calloway](../people/cole_calloway.md)**, patriarch. Arranged his daughter Birdie's marriage to settle a water dispute and genuinely cannot understand why that isn't obviously the right call. Proud, controlling, not cruel by his own lights.
- **[Hattie Calloway](../people/hattie_calloway.md)**, his wife. Wants Birdie back but won't cross Cole to say so directly — the softer entry point into this family's rivalry with the player's outfit.
- **[Whit Calloway](../people/whit_calloway.md)**, their son and heir. Stands to inherit everything Birdie walked away from and can't decide if he envies her or resents her.
- **[Birdie Calloway](../people/birdie_calloway.md)**, their daughter. Walked off the Spread rather than marry where she was told to. Now cooks and sings for [the player's outfit](the-outfit.md) instead. Whit sends her word through the fence line holding — neither of them will say plainer that they miss each other.

## Who runs it day to day

**[Jed Aldrich](../people/the_calloway_foreman.md)**, foreman, reports directly to Cole. Loyal to Cole specifically, not the family generally — and privately says his goodbyes to Birdie whenever nobody's watching, which he'd rather Cole not hear about.

**[Boyd Farrow](../people/the_calloway_ranch_hand.md)**, a hand who is, per [Sallie Voss](../people/sallie_voss.md)'s own family at [Fort Griffin](fort-griffin.md), hopelessly obvious about riding into town more than the job requires.

## Legal and financial ties

**[Emmett Cross](../people/the_calloway_water_rights_lawyer.md)** drew up the water-rights agreement and the arranged marriage tied to it — and privately thinks the marriage arrangement was a mistake, though nobody's asked him to say so formally. If Birdie ever wants to contest it, he's the one who'd know the grounds.

## Where the friction actually is

Not with the Hargroves (see [conflicts-and-rivalries.md](conflicts-and-rivalries.md)) so much as internally: a family that solved a resource dispute by trading a daughter, and is still working out what that cost them.
