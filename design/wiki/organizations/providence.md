# Providence

*See [LOCATIONS.md](../../LOCATIONS.md#providence) for the place itself — a rail-spur boomtown that may or may not get the railroad it's betting on.*

## Speculation and its counterweight

- **[Ezra Lowry](../people/ezra_lowry.md)** sells plots along a rail spur that may never get built, and genuinely believes his own pitch.
- **[Reverend Josiah Pike](../people/reverend_pike.md)** is building a church with no steeple yet, and is the town's grounded, non-cynical counterweight to Lowry — he's buried men who bought Lowry's lots and doesn't say so to be unkind.
- **[Mrs. Pruitt](../people/mrs_pruitt.md)** keeps the town's one hotel and its register of who's really passed through.
- **[The Widow Alcott](../people/widow_alcott.md)** runs a boarding house specifically for the town's unmarried working women — a plausible shared address for [Winnie Doyle](../people/winnie_doyle.md), [Louisa Okoye](../people/louisa_okoye.md), and [Cordelia Sinclair](../people/cordelia_sinclair.md), among others.
- **[Delia Frost](../people/delia_frost.md)** runs the saloon, a third distinct Providence institution alongside the hotel and boarding house.

## The press

**[Prisca Montaigne](../people/prisca_montaigne.md)** runs Providence's honest, struggling newspaper. **[Silas Cobbett](../people/the_montaigne_rival_editor.md)** runs the flashier, more sensational rival broadsheet that prints whatever sells — the specific reason Prisca's true stories keep losing the race to his exciting false ones. Neither is quite the other's enemy; Cobbett is "a little embarrassed, occasionally" rather than sorry.

**[Percival Wren](../people/percival_wren.md)**, an Eastern correspondent chasing tall tales, is a separate pressure on Prisca's honest-reporting instincts — see [Wren's own page](../people/percival_wren.md) for how his stories spread faster than facts.

## Law

**[Magistrate Holt](../people/magistrate_holt.md)** rules on the county's disputes — closing the loop on **[Temperance Vane](../people/temperance_vane.md)**'s arrests, **[Zenobia Whitmore](../people/zenobia_whitmore.md)**'s claim dispute, and **[the Widow Stroud](../people/the_widow_stroud.md)**'s debt collection when any of them actually reach a courtroom.

## Faith outside the church

**[Brother Amos](../people/brother_amos.md)** circuit-rides between Providence, Griffin, and smaller camps with no fixed church at all, and **[Padre Ruiz](../people/padre_ruiz.md)** keeps a mission chapel with a longer institutional memory of the county than Providence's newer congregation — two different answers to the same question Pike answers with a building.
