# The Hargrove Range

*See [LOCATIONS.md](../../LOCATIONS.md#the-hargrove-range) for the place itself.*

A second, newer rival ranch, deliberately distinct in flavor from the [Calloway Spread](calloway-spread.md): where the Calloways are established and tidy, Hargrove ground is recently claimed and still being proven — fresh fence wire, a half-finished barn, more debt than the buildings show. The friction here runs on water rights and overreach, not old-family pride.

## Who runs it

- **[Reed Hargrove](../people/reed_hargrove.md)**, owner. Ambitious, impatient, genuinely believes he's owed the shortcut he's about to take. Thirty years behind the Calloways and doesn't have thirty years to make up the gap patiently.
- **[Cass Hargrove](../people/cass_hargrove.md)**, his sister and equal partner. The actual brains of the operation — she collects on what Reed's ambition writes checks against, and deals fairly if the deal is genuinely good for her outfit.
- **[Suze Kirwan](../people/the_hargrove_foreman.md)**, foreman. Extremely competent and privately unimpressed by Reed's confidence — she's the actual reason the outfit functions despite him. Loyal to whoever pays on time, which today is the Hargroves.
- **[Milo Pratt](../people/milo_pratt.md)**, a hand who didn't sign on to run a decent outfit off good water and is quietly looking for a way to ride for someone else — the one sympathetic crack in this outfit's otherwise self-interested operation.

## The rest of the crew

- **[Ottilie Kraft](../people/hargrove_cook_ottilie.md)**, the outfit's cook, feeds the crew on a budget that gets tighter every month Reed's ambition outruns the ledger.
- **[Tam Wexford](../people/hargrove_wrangler_tam.md)**, horse wrangler, has quietly decided his loyalty runs to Cass rather than Reed — the same fault line **[Milo Pratt](../people/milo_pratt.md)** sits on for different reasons.
- **[Esme Radcliffe](../people/hargrove_line_rider_esme.md)** patrols the disputed water boundary on the ground, the hand this outfit would actually cross paths with before **[Beatrix Lowe](../people/beatrix_lowe.md)** or **[Matilda Greaves](../people/matilda_greaves.md)**'s survey ever reaches Reed or Cass directly.
- **[Owen Balfour](../people/hargrove_trader_owen.md)** trades cattle and gear off the books whenever the outfit is short on cash, a discreet back-channel neither Reed nor Cass officially knows about.
- **[Delia Marsh](../people/delia_marsh.md)** rides fence and night-guards water, close enough to the books to know the spread is mortgaged past what its half-built barn shows — she's stayed loyal out of pay owed rather than conviction, and is watching [the Outfit](the-outfit.md) for a fairer offer, the same kind of crack in this outfit's loyalty as **[Milo Pratt](../people/milo_pratt.md)**'s.

## Where the friction actually is

Both **[Beatrix Lowe](../people/beatrix_lowe.md)** and **[Matilda Greaves](../people/matilda_greaves.md)** got pulled into surveying the same disputed water-rights stretch this outfit is fighting the Calloways over — see [conflicts-and-rivalries.md](conflicts-and-rivalries.md) for how that resolves without either surveyor taking a side. **[Ada Mercer](../people/ada_mercer.md)** has clocked that this outfit "runs good machinery on bad debt" and can fix one of those two problems, just not the one they're usually asking about.
