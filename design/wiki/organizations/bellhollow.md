# Bellhollow

*See [LOCATIONS.md](../../LOCATIONS.md#bellhollow) for the place itself — a near-abandoned mining camp where a bell rings in the collapsed workings when nobody's there to ring it.*

Two holdouts remain, and they're the entire reason the camp isn't fully empty:

- **[Gideon Stark](../people/gideon_stark.md)**, who won't leave the claim and gives evasive, riddling answers about the bell rather than a straight one.
- **[Eulalie Stark](../people/the_stark_widow_sister.md)**, his sister, who won't leave *him* — she keeps one window lit so he can find his way back from the workings, and admits plainly what he won't: she doesn't know either.

**[The Bellhollow bell-ringer](../people/bellhollow_bell.md)** itself is deliberately left unresolved — whether it's a person, a warning, or a mistake is an open question for whoever eventually builds this room, and the Starks' evasiveness is the content, not a placeholder for a lore dump.
