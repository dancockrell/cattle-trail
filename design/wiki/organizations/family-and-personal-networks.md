# Family and personal networks

Blood, mentorship, and old history that don't fit neatly under an organization.

## The Calloway family

Covered fully on [the Calloway Spread](calloway-spread.md)'s own page — Cole, Hattie, Whit, and Birdie, plus the outfit's foreman, a hand sweet on a Voss daughter, and the family's own lawyer.

## The Reyes family

**[Josefina "Fina" Reyes](../people/fina_reyes.md)**'s father, **[Tomas Reyes](../people/tomas_reyes.md)**, is a respected vaquero at a neighboring outfit who taught her the rope and (he'll deny it) the temper too.

## The Marchetti family

**[Augustine Marchetti](../people/augustine_marchetti.md)**, a traveling dentist, and **[Opaline Marchetti](../people/opaline_marchetti.md)**, a silversmith, are related (sister or cousin, left deliberately unspecified) — Opaline chose a different trade from the family profession entirely, which is left as a detail for a future writer to develop if it becomes useful.

## The Okafor network

**[Vashti Okafor](../people/vashti_okafor.md)**, a hunter and trapper, has an aunt, **[Dr. Adaeze Okafor](../people/dr_okafor_the_elder.md)**, an accomplished physician who once offered to train her in "a real profession" and has made her peace with Vashti choosing otherwise. **[Justina Okafor](../people/justina_okafor.md)**, the county's brand inspector, shares the surname — deliberately left as an open, unresolved possible family thread rather than confirmed or denied either way.

## The Teague household

**[Orin Teague](../people/orin_teague.md)**, blacksmith, his wife **[Marta](../people/the_hollis_teague_mother.md)** (who wants his rivalry with [Naomi Freeman](../people/naomi_freeman.md) to end), and his apprentice **[Hollis](../people/hollis_teague.md)** (who privately admires Naomi's work more than his own master's) — see [Conflicts and rivalries](conflicts-and-rivalries.md) for the rivalry itself.

## The Ochoa household

**[Elena Ochoa](../people/elena_ochoa.md)**, a homesteading widow, and her son **[Tobias](../people/the_ochoa_boy.md)** — written and treated the same careful, non-romantic way as [Tam](../people/little_tam.md) and [Little Pim](../people/the_boucher_apprentice.md): present in the world, never romance-eligible, never to be added to `characters.json`.

## Willow Byrne's old circuit

**[Willow Byrne](../people/willow_byrne.md)**'s life before the outfit connects two people at [Fort Griffin](fort-griffin.md): **[Hiram Cobb](../people/hiram_cobb.md)**, her semi-retired former bounty-circuit partner who still asks if she's coming back, and **[Deacon Marsh](../people/deacon_marsh.md)**, who sold her the dead man's paper she still owes him a conversation about.

## Teachers and mentors

- **[Professor Nkemdirim](../people/the_oduya_teacher.md)** taught **[Seraphina Oduya](../people/seraphina_oduya.md)** formal medicine, and is the source of the professional bias her own arc has her grow past.
- **[Old Ansel Vogt](../people/the_falk_falconry_mentor.md)** taught **[Artemisia Falk](../people/artemisia_falk.md)** falconry and is proud his student surpassed him.
- **[Granny Combs](../people/the_combs_grandmother.md)** started the quilt-memory tradition **[Loretta Combs](../people/loretta_combs.md)** now carries on, and is a plausible source of the county's oldest, least-recorded stories.
- **[Captain Ines Fournier](../people/the_riverboat_captain.md)** was **[Josephine Merriweather](../people/josephine_merriweather.md)**'s riverboat captain before both left the river trade.
- **[Sister Adaeze Okoye](../people/sister_okoye.md)** is **[Louisa Okoye](../people/louisa_okoye.md)**'s younger sister, still waiting at their original destination town — giving Louisa's arc a clock without forcing an ending.

## Apprentices who aren't romance-eligible

**[Little Pim](../people/the_boucher_apprentice.md)** (Temperance Boucher's cooperage apprentice) and **[Tobias Ochoa](../people/the_ochoa_boy.md)** and **[Tam](../people/little_tam.md)** are all written under the same explicit rule: present, consistent, clearly childlike, never romance material, never to be added to `characters.json` under any circumstance.
