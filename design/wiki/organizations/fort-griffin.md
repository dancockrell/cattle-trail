# Fort Griffin

*See [LOCATIONS.md](../../LOCATIONS.md#fort-griffin) for the place itself.*

A hide-and-soldier town, loud, crowded, and largely indifferent to what the outfit has seen out on the grass. Its institutions are where the outfit sells, resupplies, and hears rumors.

## Law and the garrison

- **[Sheriff Amos Dell](../people/amos_dell.md)** keeps the peace inside the town line and deliberately stays out of anything past it — "whatever happened out past the fork stays past the fork."
- **[Captain Dunwoody](../people/captain_dunwoody.md)** commands the garrison, answers to orders from further away than the county understands, and keeps his soldiers out of local disputes as a matter of policy.
- **[Corporal Ness](../people/corporal_ness.md)** is Dunwoody's bored, talkative off-duty source of troop-post gossip.
- **[Territorial Marshal Odom](../people/marshal_odom.md)** has the reach Dell doesn't — federal jurisdiction, rarely exercised, and the officer who deputized **[Temperance Vane](../people/temperance_vane.md)** because she does the job right.
- **[Judge Abernathy](../people/the_thorne_customer.md)**, retired circuit judge, now a regular customer of **[Hepzibah Thorne](../people/hepzibah_thorne.md)**'s clockmaking and a source of older county history that predates the currently-serving **[Magistrate Holt](../people/magistrate_holt.md)** at [Providence](providence.md).

## Trade

- **[Nettie Voss](../people/nettie_voss.md)** runs the general trading post; her daughter **[Sallie](../people/sallie_voss.md)** is learning the trade and is the reason **[Boyd Farrow](../people/the_calloway_ranch_hand.md)** rides into town more than his job at the [Calloway Spread](calloway-spread.md) strictly requires.
- **[Consuelo Diaz](../people/consuelo_diaz.md)** runs the hide and freight side of the market, distinct from Voss's general goods.
- **[Deacon Marsh](../people/deacon_marsh.md)** brokers bounty paper — including the dead man's paper he once sold **[Willow Byrne](../people/willow_byrne.md)**, a conversation she still owes him.
- **[The Widow Stroud](../people/the_widow_stroud.md)** lends money to outfits that fall behind, unfailingly polite and utterly unsentimental about the terms; her clerk **[Mortimer Vance](../people/the_stroud_clerk.md)** is visibly uncomfortable delivering her collection notices in person.
- **[Ellison Rhodes](../people/ellison_rhodes.md)**, a former cavalry sergeant, guides independently out of Griffin.

## Medicine

**[Dr. Rafael Alvarez](../people/doc_alvarez.md)** is the nearest doctor — practical, unshockable, a grounded counterpart to [Eleanor](../people/eleanor.md)'s spirit-healing and to the newer, more specialized medical companions ([Marguerite Solis](../people/marguerite_solis.md), [Augustine Marchetti](../people/augustine_marchetti.md), [Dinah Okonkwo](../people/dinah_okonkwo.md)) who all work the wider county.

## Communications

**[Otis Beckwith](../people/otis_beckwith.md)** manages the telegraph office and is **[Prudence Hale](../people/prudence_hale.md)**'s direct superior.
