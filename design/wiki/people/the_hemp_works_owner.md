# Cyrus Boone

**Status:** minor/supporting character (`design/npcs.json`), category **town**

Age 55. Owns the hemp works where Marisol Vasquez makes rope, and the cheaper rival who undercut her.

**Location:** unplaced

## Personality
gruff but fair, values Marisol's work correctly even when it costs him, frustrated by his own rival supplier.

## Why she/he exists
Gives Marisol Vasquez's rope-making record an actual employer/workplace rather than existing in isolation, and names the competitive pressure her companion hook references.

## Appearance
Adult man, mid-fifties, calloused hands, plain but well-run workshop visible behind him.
