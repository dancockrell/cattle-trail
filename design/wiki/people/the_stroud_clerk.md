# Mortimer Vance

**Status:** minor/supporting character (`design/npcs.json`), category **antagonist**

Age 34. The Widow Stroud's clerk, who actually delivers her collection notices in person.

**Location:** Fort Griffin

## Personality
nervous, doesn't enjoy the job, surprisingly sympathetic if approached directly.

## Why she/he exists
Softens the Widow Stroud's antagonist role with a human-scale employee who's uncomfortable with the work -- a possible source of leniency or advance warning if the outfit treats him decently.

## Appearance
Adult man, mid-thirties, ill-fitting formal clothes, visibly uncomfortable with confrontation.

## Voice
> I don't set the terms. I just deliver the paper. Please don't make me the villain of this conversation.

> Mrs. Stroud would say I'm too soft for this work. I'd agree with her.

> I could mention, unofficially, when a deadline's coming before it's official. I'm not supposed to.
