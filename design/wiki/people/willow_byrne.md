# Willow Byrne

**Status:** romance-eligible companion (`design/characters.json`) &mdash; design only

Age 24. Former bounty tracker who has stopped taking paper on the living and would rather ride escort now.

## Personality
guarded, dry-humored, watchful, loyal once earned.

## Appearance and dress
Clearly adult, ordinary but gorgeous woman: slim, small-chested with little or no prominent bust, attractive hip/seat silhouette, healthy and active. Charismatic, comfortable in herself and sexually confident. No approved art exists for this draft.

Weathered brown duster over a plain shirt and vest, gun belt worn for use rather than show, low-crowned hat pulled down, scuffed boots. Practical PG-13 coverage; nothing decorative that would catch on brush or a saddle horn.

*Art status: unapproved draft.*

## How you meet her
The rustler has a partner Willow has been quietly tracking for three counties; she rides in on the same trouble from the other direction.

**To recruit her:**
- Drive off or capture the rustler's partner without Willow having to do it alone.
- Offer her steady pay and a straight answer about what the outfit actually does.
- Willow accepts the invitation.

## What she needs
**Raises her strain:** A face she tracked turning out to already be dead.

**Recovers it:** Being trusted with the watch rather than talked at about it.

## Perk: Steady Aim
Extends the player's effective shooting range while Willow is assigned to the field.

## Her adventure: "Three Counties Behind"
**Objective:** Play as Willow reading old sign to find where her original quarry actually went, while the protagonist keeps watch on the camp she left behind.

**Her skill:** Directly playable tracking: choose the true trail among several false ones read from hoofprints and camp litter.

**Your support:** The protagonist covers her back at the cold camp she is investigating.

**Payoff:** Closing an old file out loud to someone who actually listens, and a hand held a beat longer than necessary.
