# Marlowe Castellano

**Status:** romance-eligible companion (`design/characters.json`) &mdash; design only

Age 24. A traveling actress whose theater troupe folded three towns back, leaving her stranded with real talent and nowhere to use it.

## Personality
dramatic in a way that's earned rather than performed, adaptable, reads a room instantly, surprisingly grounded offstage.

## Appearance and dress
Clearly adult, ordinary but gorgeous woman: slim, small-chested with little or no prominent bust, attractive hip/seat silhouette, healthy and active. Charismatic, comfortable in herself and sexually confident. No approved art exists for this draft.

Traveling clothes with one carefully preserved good dress for when she needs to be someone else briefly, a small trunk of costume pieces.

*Art status: unapproved draft.*

## How you meet her
Her troupe's manager absconded with the takings at the last stop, leaving her and a wagon of costumes with nowhere to go and no way to get there.

**To recruit her:**
- See through one of her performances and call it correctly.
- Give her an audience, even a small one.
- Marlowe accepts the invitation.

## What she needs
**Raises her strain:** Being unable to tell anymore which version of herself is real.

**Recovers it:** Being wanted for who she is offstage, not the character.

## Her adventure: "The Role She Didn't Choose"
**Objective:** Play as Marlowe talking her way past a genuinely dangerous situation using performance and misdirection rather than a weapon.

**Her skill:** Directly playable persuasion/performance beats with real stakes, not a dialogue-wheel bluff.

**Your support:** The protagonist plays along with whatever role she assigns them, on faith.

**Payoff:** A private, unperformed moment afterward, deliberately contrasted with the show she just gave.
