# Nell Corrigan

**Status:** romance-eligible companion (`design/characters.json`) &mdash; design only

Age 26. Her family ran the boarding house at Bellhollow back when the mine still paid; when it played out and everyone else left, she stayed to keep the bell the old miners swore marked safe passage through the workings, and to keep company with whoever it is that still rings it when no living hand is near.

## Personality
unbothered by things that would rattle anyone else, hospitable, keeps her own counsel, finds comfort in routine and a shared meal.

## Appearance and dress
Clearly adult, ordinary but gorgeous woman: slim, small-chested with little or no prominent bust, attractive hip/seat silhouette, healthy and active. Charismatic, comfortable in herself and sexually confident. No approved art exists for this draft.

A practical housedress with a shawl kept close against the hill country's night chill, a ring of old mine keys at her belt she never explains, boots suited to uneven ground.

## How you meet her
The outfit rides through Bellhollow for shelter from a storm, and she has supper on before anyone's finished tying off the horses -- she was expecting somebody, though not necessarily them.

**To recruit her:**
- Sit through supper and don't flinch when the bell rings on its own.
- Ask her about the bell only if you actually want the honest answer.
- Nell accepts the invitation.

## What she needs
**Raises her strain:** The night the bell doesn't ring at all.

**Recovers it:** Sitting up with her at the bell tower until whatever's restless settles.

## Perk: Camp Song
Reuses the existing camp-rest perk definition; supper and an old miner's song at Bellhollow settle a camp the way nothing rehearsed could.

## Her adventure: "The Bell That Rings Itself"
**Objective:** Play as Nell climbing into the collapsed workings to find and clear whatever's fouling the bell rope before the next storm brings the rest of the headframe down.

**Her skill:** Directly playable careful climbing and rope-clearing in tight, unstable ground, not a horror set-piece.

**Your support:** The protagonist braces the rope from below and calls out the moment something shifts.

**Payoff:** The bell rings clean on its own that night, and she tells the protagonist, plainly, that it's the first time in years she hasn't minded somebody hearing her talk back to it.
