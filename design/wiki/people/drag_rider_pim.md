# Pim Sorensen

**Status:** minor/supporting character (`design/npcs.json`), category **outfit_hand**

Age 21. Rides drag, working the dusty rear of the drive and catching stragglers.

**Location:** Clear Fork

## Personality
stubborn, coughs more than he complains, takes the worst job on the drive without being asked twice, wants Pardo's respect.

## Why she/he exists
Rides drag -- the choking dust at the back of the herd, catching every calf and lame steer that falls behind. The unglamorous job that keeps the count honest, which is the whole reason the outfit exists.

## Appearance
Adult man in his early twenties, perpetually dust-caked, kerchief pulled up over his nose and mouth.

## Voice

> Lost count's a lost sale. I don't lose count.

> Pardo says I'll make point rider by autumn. I'm holding him to it.
