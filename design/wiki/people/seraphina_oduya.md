## How you meet her
She arrives at a crisis at the same time as Marguerite Solis, initially clashing over method until the emergency forces them to combine both approaches to save the patient.

**To recruit her:**
- Watch her and Marguerite Solis actually work together instead of competing.
- Take her formal training seriously without dismissing folk method either.
- Seraphina accepts the invitation.

## What she needs
**Raises her strain:** Being proven wrong about a method she was certain of, in front of someone she'd dismissed.

**Recovers it:** Being treated as a colleague once trust is actually earned, not assumed from credentials.
