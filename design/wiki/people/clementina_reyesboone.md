# Clementina Reyes-Boone

**Status:** romance-eligible companion (`design/characters.json`) &mdash; design only

Age 27. Runs the only reliable ferry crossing on a stretch of river too dangerous to ford most of the year.

## Personality
unflappable, reads water like others read a room, protective of the crossing's safety record, dry humor about travelers who think they know better.

## Appearance and dress
Clearly adult, ordinary but gorgeous woman: slim, small-chested with little or no prominent bust, attractive hip/seat silhouette, healthy and active. Charismatic, comfortable in herself and sexually confident. No approved art exists for this draft.

Practical, waterproofed work clothes, strong capable build from years of poling and rowing, calm watchful manner.

*Art status: unapproved draft.*

## How you meet her
A cheaper, unsafe rival ferry operator is undercutting her prices while ignoring the water conditions that make her own careful timing necessary, risking someone's life to save an hour.

**To recruit her:**
- Trust her read of the water over your own impatience.
- Help her defend her crossing's exclusive rights from an unsafe competitor.
- Clementina accepts the invitation.

## What she needs
**Raises her strain:** A crossing attempted at the wrong time that could have been prevented by patience.

**Recovers it:** Quiet, watchful company on the water.

## Perk: Between the Footprints
Reuses the existing shared trail-hazard perk definition rather than authoring a duplicate -- reading dangerous water works the same as reading dangerous trail.

## Her adventure: "The Crossing That Couldn't Wait"
**Objective:** Play as Clementina safely navigating a genuinely dangerous crossing at exactly the right moment, timing everything against the water's real behavior.

**Her skill:** Directly playable timing-and-read-the-water beats under real stakes.

**Your support:** The protagonist manages the cargo and passengers while she reads the current.

**Payoff:** A safe crossing made exactly when everyone else said it was impossible, and her calm, hard-earned confidence.
