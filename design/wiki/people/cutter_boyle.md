# Cutter Boyle

**Status:** minor/supporting character (`design/npcs.json`), category **antagonist**

Age 40. A hired gun working the edges of the county for whoever pays.

**Location:** unplaced

## Personality
businesslike about violence, not sadistic, has walked away from jobs before.

## Why she/he exists
A mercenary antagonist distinct from Silas's desperation -- Boyle isn't cornered, he's a professional, which makes him negotiable in a way a cornered man isn't. Useful if a later encounter needs an antagonist who can be talked out of a job rather than only fought.

## Appearance
Adult man, forty, plain trail gear built for function, no flash on the gun rig.

## Voice
> I don't get paid enough to hate you personally. Take that as comfort or don't.

> Somebody's always willing to pay more. Ask them first before you ask me to stop.

> I've walked off three jobs this year. This one's not decided yet.
