# Juniper Holloway

**Status:** romance-eligible companion (`design/characters.json`) &mdash; design only

Age 22. A tea and coffee roaster whose small trade keeps half the county's mornings functional.

## Personality
warm, detail-oriented about flavor and freshness, quietly competitive about quality, generous with a good cup.

## Appearance and dress
Clearly adult, ordinary but gorgeous woman: slim, small-chested with little or no prominent bust, attractive hip/seat silhouette, healthy and active. Charismatic, comfortable in herself and sexually confident. No approved art exists for this draft.

Practical work clothes marked faintly by roasting smoke, an apron with pockets for sample scoops, warm unhurried manner.

## How you meet her
Her regular shipment of beans is stuck at a washed-out crossing and about to spoil in the heat, threatening her whole season's supply.

**To recruit her:**
- Help her recover a shipment of beans damaged or delayed in transit.
- Actually taste the difference in her work instead of treating coffee as interchangeable.
- Juniper accepts the invitation.

## What she needs
**Raises her strain:** A carefully sourced batch ruined by delay or poor storage.

**Recovers it:** Someone actually noticing the difference good work makes in something ordinary.

**Objective:** Play as Juniper rescuing a spoiling shipment against time and heat, prioritizing what can still be saved.
