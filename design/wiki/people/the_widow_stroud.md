# The Widow Stroud

**Status:** minor/supporting character (`design/npcs.json`), category **antagonist**

Age 51. A moneylender operating out of Fort Griffin with an interest in outfits that fall behind.

**Location:** Fort Griffin

## Personality
unfailingly polite, utterly unsentimental about debt, patient because patience compounds interest.

## Why she/he exists
A non-violent antagonist: financial pressure rather than a gun. If the outfit ever needs money fast, she's who lends it, and she never needs to threaten anyone -- the terms do that on their own.

## Appearance
Adult woman, early fifties, dressed well but not ostentatiously, composed in a way that reads as controlled rather than warm.

## Voice
> I don't want your herd. I want what your herd is worth, on the date we agreed.

> Interest doesn't care whether your trip was hard. Neither, regrettably, do I.

> I've never once raised my voice collecting a debt. I've rarely needed to twice.
