# Marisol Vasquez

**Status:** romance-eligible companion (`design/characters.json`) &mdash; design only

Age 21. A rope-maker at a small hemp works, whose lines the local outfits specifically request by name.

## Personality
cheerful, physically strong and unbothered by it, direct, takes real pride in a rope that holds.

## Appearance and dress
Clearly adult, ordinary but gorgeous woman: slim, small-chested with little or no prominent bust, attractive hip/seat silhouette, healthy and active. Charismatic, comfortable in herself and sexually confident. No approved art exists for this draft.

Practical work clothes suited to heavy manual labor, calloused hands, a coil of her own rope worn like a badge.

*Art status: unapproved draft.*

## How you meet her
A cheaper rope-maker undercutting her prices sold the outfit a line that failed at a bad moment, and she offers to make it right for free to prove the difference.

**To recruit her:**
- Test one of her ropes under real strain and admit it held.
- Order from her instead of a cheaper competitor.
- Marisol accepts the invitation.

## What she needs
**Raises her strain:** A rope failing and someone getting hurt because of it.

**Recovers it:** Physical work that proves something rather than words that claim it.

## Perk: Trail Hand
Reuses the existing rancher-family perk definition rather than authoring a duplicate -- her ropes are the reason the trick works.

## Her adventure: "The Rope That Didn't Break"
**Objective:** Play as Marisol demonstrating her rope's quality under a real, dangerous load to win back the outfit's trust in her craft.

**Her skill:** Directly playable strength-and-technique beats, not a stat check disguised as a cutscene.

**Your support:** The protagonist rigs the test and calls the results honestly either way.

**Payoff:** The rope holds, obviously, and she lets herself gloat exactly once.
