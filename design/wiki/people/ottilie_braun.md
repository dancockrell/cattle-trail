meticulous, quietly proud, burns herself more than she'll admit, finds heat and pressure soothing.

## How you meet her
The outfit's lanterns are cracked and she's the only glassworker within reach who can actually fix rather than replace them.

**To recruit her:**
- Recover a shipment of lamp glass damaged in transit.
- Trust her repair over buying new.
- Ottilie accepts the invitation.

## What she needs
**Raises her strain:** Being told glasswork is a hobby, not a trade.

**Recovers it:** A quiet workshop and nobody rushing her flame.

**Objective:** Play as Ottilie reworking a cracked lantern glass under real heat and timing constraints, not a menu-based repair.

**Her skill:** Directly playable glasswork: timing, temperature, a steady hand.

**Payoff:** A lantern that finally holds, and her visible relief when it does.
