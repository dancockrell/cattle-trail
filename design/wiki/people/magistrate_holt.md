# Magistrate Holt

**Status:** minor/supporting character (`design/npcs.json`), category **town**

Age 57. Rules on the legal disputes Marshal Odom's arrests and Zenobia's claim disputes end up in front of.

**Location:** Providence

## Personality
formal, genuinely tries to be fair, privately exhausted by how often frontier law is improvised.

## Why she/he exists
Closes the loop on several existing hooks (Temperance Vane's arrests, Zenobia Whitmore's claim, the Widow Stroud's debt collection) by giving the county an actual court rather than leaving legal outcomes implied.

## Appearance
Adult man, late fifties, plain but formal dress, tired but attentive expression.

## Voice
> The law out here is half book, half best judgment. I try to keep the ratio honest.

> I've upheld Widow Stroud's contracts and I've thrown two of them out. She respects the second more than the first.

> A claim dispute needs paper, not just conviction. Bring me paper.
