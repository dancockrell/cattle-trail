# Amos Ketch

**Status:** minor/supporting character (`design/npcs.json`), category **antagonist**

Age 50. A third, more genuinely unpleasant independent trail-crew boss, contrasting Zilpah Hendricks's fairness.

**Location:** unplaced

## Personality
exploitative toward his own crew, outwardly friendly to outfits he wants something from, the actual bad-faith operator the Hargroves and Calloways are not.

## Why she/he exists
The county has two large, morally-mixed-but-not-villainous rival outfits (Calloway, Hargrove) and one small, fair independent (Zilpah). Ketch fills a real antagonist niche: an operator who actually treats people badly, giving the setting one clear bad actor without making either rival ranch flatly evil.

## Appearance
Adult man, fifty, superficially affable, harder eyes than his manner suggests.

## Voice
> My crew works when I say and eats what's left over. That's the arrangement. They knew that going in.

> Hendricks runs her outfit soft. Soft doesn't survive out here. We'll see whose crew is still riding in a year.

> I'm friendly to whoever's useful to me today. Ask me again tomorrow if that's still you.
