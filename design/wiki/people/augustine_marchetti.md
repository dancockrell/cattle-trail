# Augustine Marchetti

**Status:** romance-eligible companion (`design/characters.json`) &mdash; design only

Age 25. A traveling dentist whose actual medical training outpaces most patients' expectations of the trade.

## Personality
brisk, surprisingly gentle for someone with pliers, no patience for quack tonics sold as cures, confident in a very specific competence.

Clearly adult, ordinary but gorgeous woman: slim, small-chested with little or no prominent bust, attractive hip/seat silhouette, healthy and active. Charismatic, comfortable in herself and sexually confident. No approved art exists for this draft.

## How you meet her
She's the only dentist for two counties and the outfit's own trail cook is in genuine pain when their paths cross.

**To recruit her:**
- Trust her over a faster, worse remedy for a real toothache.
- Sit still through an actually competent procedure.
- Augustine accepts the invitation.

## What she needs
**Raises her strain:** Watching someone suffer needlessly because they distrust real medicine in favor of a quack cure.

**Recovers it:** Being trusted through something that hurts before it helps.
