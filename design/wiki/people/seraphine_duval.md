# Seraphine Duval

**Status:** romance-eligible companion (`design/characters.json`) &mdash; design only

Age 23. A perfumer and soap-maker whose small luxuries are treasured out of proportion to their size.

## Personality
sensory and precise, surprisingly business-minded, generous with samples, finds calm in scent the way others find it in silence.

## How you meet her
A shipment of delicate oils she needs for her trade was damaged crossing bad water, and she needs help finding a substitute source before her whole stock goes rancid.

**To recruit her:**
- Help her recover ingredients lost or spoiled in transit.
- Actually notice and appreciate a scent she's made rather than dismissing it as frivolous.
- Seraphine accepts the invitation.

## What she needs
**Raises her strain:** A carefully blended scent ruined by careless storage.

**Recovers it:** Someone noticing the small, deliberate details she puts into her work.

## Her adventure: "The Scent That Almost Spoiled"
**Objective:** Play as Seraphine finding and testing substitute ingredients under time pressure before her stock spoils entirely.

**Her skill:** Directly playable sensory-matching beats: identify the right substitute by scent and quality, not guesswork.

**Your support:** The protagonist fetches candidate ingredients from local sources while she tests them.

**Payoff:** A salvaged batch, and a scent she blends specifically to remind the protagonist of this trip.
