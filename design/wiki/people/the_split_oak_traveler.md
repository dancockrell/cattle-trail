# The man from farther west

**Status:** minor/supporting character (`design/npcs.json`), category **wanderer**

Age not stated. A recurring, never-named drifter met at Split Oak Camp.

**Location:** Split Oak Camp

## Personality
deliberately unnamed and slightly unplaceable, not unfriendly, carries news from further than anyone else the outfit meets.

## Why she/he exists
This is the rider referenced in LORE.md's rumor list: 'a rider from farther west said the sky over the ocean had been wrong for a week. Nobody here needed the rest of that story.' Written here as a recurring figure specifically so he can be met more than once without a name pinning him down -- per LORE.md, nobody in Clear Fork should ever need or get the rest of that story.

## Appearance
Adult man, indeterminate age, road-worn gear that doesn't obviously belong to any one region.

## Voice
> Sky was wrong out past the coast for a week. Didn't ask why. Wasn't going to like the answer.

> I carry news, not opinions about it. Take the first, leave the second.

> Same fire, different trail every time you meet me. That's the arrangement.

## Notes
- Not implemented in scripts/. Deliberately unnamed -- do not assign him a proper name in a future pass without a specific decision to do so; the anonymity is load-bearing for LORE.md's 'do not name the Japan thing' rule by proxy.
