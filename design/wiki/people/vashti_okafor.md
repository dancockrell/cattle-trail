**Status:** romance-eligible companion (`design/characters.json`) &mdash; design only

Age 20. A hunter and trapper who feeds a camp better than any outfit's usual rations.

## Personality
quiet, extremely competent outdoors, unimpressed by bravado, generous with what she catches.

## Appearance and dress
Clearly adult, ordinary but gorgeous woman: slim, small-chested with little or no prominent bust, attractive hip/seat silhouette, healthy and active. Charismatic, comfortable in herself and sexually confident. No approved art exists for this draft.

Fringed practical leathers built for moving quiet through brush, a well-used bow and knife, no wasted decoration.

## How you meet her
She's tracking the same game the outfit startled off its usual ground, and isn't happy about the competition until she sees they're not wasteful.

**To recruit her:**
- Track something the outfit couldn't find alone.
- Don't waste what she brings back.
- Vashti accepts the invitation.

## What she needs
**Raises her strain:** Waste -- a kill or a resource thrown away.

**Recovers it:** Being handed a task instead of being watched work.

## Perk: Between the Footprints
Reuses the existing shared trail-hazard perk definition rather than authoring a duplicate -- a hunter's eye for sign works the same as a scout's.

## Her adventure: "What the Ground Remembers"
**Objective:** Play as Vashti tracking a specific animal across mixed terrain, reading sign the protagonist can't see.

**Her skill:** Directly playable tracking: read hoofprints, broken brush, scent direction.

**Your support:** The protagonist carries gear and stays downwind on her instruction.

**Payoff:** A clean, respectful kill and a shared meal that night, not a trophy.
