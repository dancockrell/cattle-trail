# Percival Wren

**Status:** minor/supporting character (`design/npcs.json`), category **wanderer**

Age 37. A traveling newspaper correspondent chasing tall-tale stories for an Eastern paper.

**Location:** unplaced

## Personality
credulous in print, skeptical in person, genuinely curious, will print the exciting version regardless of what actually happened.

## Why she/he exists
A comic-tinged wanderer who turns the outfit's real, mundane hardships into lurid tabloid copy -- a lens on how the Weird West gets flattened into myth even while it's happening, without the game itself needing to endorse that flattening.

## Appearance
Adult man, late thirties, city clothes increasingly worn by trail conditions, notebook always out.

## Voice
> A rustler and some spooked cattle. My editor will have it as a gunfight with the walking dead by Friday.

> I don't need it to be true. I need it to sell papers in Boston.

> Tell me the story again, and this time include a monster. My readers expect a monster.
