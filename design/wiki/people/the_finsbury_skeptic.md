# Reeve Talmadge

**Status:** minor/supporting character (`design/npcs.json`), category **antagonist**

Age 44. A prominent skeptic publicly mocking Electra Finsbury's electrical experiments as dangerous nonsense.

**Location:** unplaced

## Personality
condescending, genuinely afraid of what he doesn't understand, masked as scorn, influential enough locally to actually hurt her reputation.

## Why she/he exists
A social/reputational antagonist for Electra Finsbury rather than a physical threat -- public ridicule that could cost her the trust and funding she needs to keep working.

## Appearance
Adult man, mid-forties, self-important formal dress, performs authority he doesn't fully possess.
