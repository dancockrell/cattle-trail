# Zilpah Hendricks

**Status:** romance-eligible companion (`design/characters.json`) &mdash; design only

Age 28. Runs her own small independent trail crew, older and more established than Dorothea Farrow's fledgling operation, and could mentor or compete with her.

## Personality
commanding without being harsh, protective of her small crew, pragmatic about alliances, respects competence over reputation.

## Appearance and dress
Clearly adult, ordinary but gorgeous woman: slim, small-chested with little or no prominent bust, attractive hip/seat silhouette, healthy and active. Charismatic, comfortable in herself and sexually confident. No approved art exists for this draft.

Practical, well-worn trail-boss gear, a bearing that reads as authority without needing to assert it, gear chosen for function over show.

*Art status: unapproved draft.*

## How you meet her
Her small crew and the player's outfit end up working the same difficult stretch of trail at the same time, and cooperation serves both better than competition would.

**To recruit her:**
- Work alongside her crew as an equal, not a rival.
- Prove trustworthy with a genuine, mutual arrangement.
- Zilpah accepts the invitation.

## What she needs
**Raises her strain:** A crew member hurt or lost because of a decision she made.

**Recovers it:** Being trusted as an equal partner rather than folded into someone else's outfit.

## Her adventure: "The Stretch That Needed Two Crews"
**Objective:** Play as Zilpah coordinating two crews through a genuinely difficult stretch of trail that neither could safely manage alone.

**Her skill:** Directly playable coordination-and-leadership beats: her crew and the player's outfit both have to actually cooperate.

**Your support:** The protagonist trusts her read of the terrain and defers leadership of the joint effort to her.

**Payoff:** Both crews through safely, and her rare, direct acknowledgment that the partnership worked.
