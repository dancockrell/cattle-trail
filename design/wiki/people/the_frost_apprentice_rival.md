# Cato Villanueva

**Status:** minor/supporting character (`design/npcs.json`), category **antagonist**

Age 26. A rival sign painter, less skilled but more aggressive about undercutting Wilhelmina Frost's commissions.

**Location:** unplaced

## Personality
insecure about his own lesser skill, aggressive in business rather than physically, occasionally recognizes his own mediocrity in quiet moments.

## Why she/he exists
A low-stakes professional rival for Wilhelmina Frost, purely competitive rather than dangerous, giving her trade some ongoing texture.

## Appearance
Adult man, mid-twenties, paint-stained but noticeably sloppier work than Wilhelmina's, restless competitive energy.

## Voice
> Faster and cheaper. That's the whole pitch. Nobody's asking for a masterpiece over a storefront.

> Frost paints slower. Frost also doesn't have half her signs peeling after one winter, if I'm honest with myself.

> I'll underbid her again next season. I always do. She always still gets the good commissions anyway.
