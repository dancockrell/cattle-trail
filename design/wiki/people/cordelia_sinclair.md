# Cordelia Sinclair

**Status:** romance-eligible companion (`design/characters.json`) &mdash; design only

Age 26. A traveling librarian who carries a genuine, if small, collection from town to town by wagon.

## Personality
warm, endlessly curious, protective of her books past the point of sense, gently insistent that everyone deserves to read something.

## Appearance and dress
Clearly adult, ordinary but gorgeous woman: slim, small-chested with little or no prominent bust, attractive hip/seat silhouette, healthy and active. Charismatic, comfortable in herself and sexually confident. No approved art exists for this draft.

Practical traveling dress with deep pockets for a notebook, spectacles, ink-stained fingers, a wagon rigged with careful shelving.

*Art status: unapproved draft.*

## How you meet her
Her book wagon breaks an axle at the worst possible moment, with a storm coming and a collection she cannot replace inside it.

**To recruit her:**
- Help her protect her collection from a real threat -- weather, theft or worse.
- Actually borrow and return something.
- Cordelia accepts the invitation.

## What she needs
**Raises her strain:** A book damaged or lost that can't be replaced out here.

**Recovers it:** Being read to, or reading to someone who actually listens.

## Her adventure: "The Wagon Full of Words"
**Objective:** Play as Cordelia racing a storm to get her book wagon under cover and her collection dry.

**Her skill:** Directly playable triage-under-weather beats: what gets saved first, what can wait.

**Your support:** The protagonist hauls crates and holds the tarp steady against real wind.

**Payoff:** Everything saved, dry, and a book handed over specifically chosen for the protagonist.
