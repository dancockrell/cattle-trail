# Naomi Freeman

**Status:** romance-eligible companion (`design/characters.json`) &mdash; design only

Age 23. Freedwoman homesteader and blacksmith who took over her late father's forge and kept its custom.

## Personality
steady, proud, plainspoken, dry wit.

## Appearance and dress
Clearly adult, ordinary but gorgeous woman: slim, small-chested with little or no prominent bust, attractive hip/seat silhouette, healthy and active. Charismatic, comfortable in herself and sexually confident. No approved art exists for this draft.

Heavy canvas work apron over a rolled-sleeve shirt, sturdy trousers, hair tied back and covered against forge sparks, forearms bared from the work. Adult, capable, daring PG-13 coverage suited to smithing rather than riding.

*Art status: unapproved draft.*

## How you meet her
A cracked wagon axle strands the outfit near her homestead forge and she names a fair price to fix it right instead of fast.

**To recruit her:**
- Trust her judgment over a quicker, worse repair.
- Offer standing work rather than a one-time job.
- Naomi accepts the invitation.

## What she needs
**Raises her strain:** Being told a woman's forge work needs a man's name on it to count.

**Recovers it:** Working alongside someone rather than being fussed over.

## Perk: Sure-Handed Tuning
Reuses the existing shared repair-efficiency perk definition rather than authoring a duplicate.

## Her adventure: "The Customers Who Would Not Pay"
**Objective:** Play as Naomi facing down two men trying to take a finished wagon wheel without paying, while the protagonist backs her at the door.

**Her skill:** Directly playable plain talk and a forge hammer held with obvious competence, not violence played for spectacle.

**Your support:** The protagonist stands visibly at her back without taking the argument from her.

**Payoff:** She hands the protagonist the hammer to hold, which is trust, and says so out loud.
