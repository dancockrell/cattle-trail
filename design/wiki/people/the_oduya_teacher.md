# Professor Nkemdirim

**Status:** minor/supporting character (`design/npcs.json`), category **family**

Age 58. Seraphina Oduya's former instructor at a formal medical program east of the territory.

**Location:** unplaced

## Personality
rigorous, proud of his student, would be quietly disappointed to hear she's learned anything from 'unlicensed' practitioners.

## Why she/he exists
Gives Seraphina Oduya's formal training an actual origin and a source of the professional bias she has to grow past in her own companion arc.

## Appearance
Adult man, late fifties, formal academic dress, precise and exacting bearing softened by evident pride.

## Voice
> I taught her that instruments don't lie. I should have also taught her that hands sometimes know things instruments haven't measured yet.

> She writes asking sharper questions than most of my current students. I don't tell her that as often as I should.

> If she's genuinely learned something from a folk healer, I'd want to hear it. I'd also want to argue about it first.
