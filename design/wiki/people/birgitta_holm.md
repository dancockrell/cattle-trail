## How you meet her
The mine she works for is cutting corners on shoring that's putting the whole crew at real risk, and the owner won't listen to a miner's word over a ledger's bottom line.

**To recruit her:**
- Help her address a genuinely dangerous safety problem at her mine.
- Take her firsthand experience seriously over an owner's dismissal of it.
- Birgitta accepts the invitation.

## What she needs
**Raises her strain:** A safety warning ignored until someone actually gets hurt.

**Recovers it:** Being believed the first time instead of after something goes wrong.

## Perk: Between the Footprints
Reuses the existing shared trail-hazard perk definition rather than authoring a duplicate -- nobody reads unstable ground better than a miner.

## Her adventure: "The Shoring That Wouldn't Hold"
**Objective:** Play as Birgitta proving a mine safety hazard is real and forcing a fix before it costs someone their life.

**Her skill:** Directly playable hazard-assessment beats: read the actual structural risk, then make the case undeniably.

**Your support:** The protagonist backs her assessment publicly in front of a skeptical owner.

**Payoff:** The shoring fixed, her crew safer, and her rare admission that having someone believe her mattered more than winning the argument.
