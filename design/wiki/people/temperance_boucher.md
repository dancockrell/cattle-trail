## How you meet her
A batch of her barrels failed under unusual pressure conditions and her reputation is on the line until she can prove the fault was in the conditions, not her craft.

**To recruit her:**
- Bring her a barrel-sealing problem that actually challenges her.
- Trust her cooperage over a cheaper, worse container.
- Temperance accepts the invitation.

## What she needs
**Raises her strain:** Her craft blamed for a failure that wasn't actually her fault.

**Recovers it:** Someone believing her diagnosis of what actually went wrong before she has to prove it twice.
