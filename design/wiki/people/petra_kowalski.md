# Petra Kowalski

**Status:** romance-eligible companion (`design/characters.json`) &mdash; design only

Age 22. A saddler and leatherworker whose gear outlasts everyone else's by years.

## Personality
methodical, quietly competitive about craftsmanship, warm once she stops sizing you up, particular about good leather.

## Appearance and dress
Clearly adult, ordinary but gorgeous woman: slim, small-chested with little or no prominent bust, attractive hip/seat silhouette, healthy and active. Charismatic, comfortable in herself and sexually confident. No approved art exists for this draft.

A heavy work apron over practical clothes, tools at her belt, hands permanently marked by awl and needle.

*Art status: unapproved draft.*

## How you meet her
The outfit's saddle gear is failing at the worst possible moment and she's the only saddler within reach who does it right instead of fast.

**To recruit her:**
- Bring her a saddle or harness worth repairing properly.
- Don't rush her past the point where the work would fail later.
- Petra accepts the invitation.

## What she needs
**Raises her strain:** Gear that fails in the field because someone cut corners making it.

**Recovers it:** Working alongside someone rather than being watched.

## Perk: Sure-Handed Tuning
Reuses the existing shared repair-efficiency perk definition rather than authoring a duplicate.

## Her adventure: "The Saddle That Had to Hold"
**Objective:** Play as Petra rebuilding a failing saddle rig correctly under time pressure, refusing a faster worse fix even when pushed.

**Her skill:** Directly playable careful leatherwork beats: measure twice, stitch once, no shortcuts rewarded.

**Your support:** The protagonist holds the frame steady and fetches the right tool each time, learning the trade a little.

**Payoff:** A saddle that holds through the hardest test available, and her quiet pride in it.
