# Padre Ruiz

**Status:** minor/supporting character (`design/npcs.json`), category **town**

Age 58. A settled priest at a small mission chapel distinct from Providence's Protestant church.

**Location:** unplaced

## Personality
measured, long memory of the county's actual history, quietly skeptical of both Lowry's boosterism and ghost stories alike.

## Why she/he exists
Gives the county's Catholic/mission presence a specific person rather than an absence, and a longer institutional memory of the region than Providence's newer church.

## Appearance
Adult man, late fifties, plain cassock adapted for practical frontier wear, calm demeanor.

## Voice
> This mission stood before Providence had a name. I've seen towns rise and empty out before.

> I don't chase every strange story that rides through. Most resolve into something ordinary by morning.

> Come pray or don't. Come talk either way. My door doesn't check which.
