# Roisin Dunmore

**Status:** romance-eligible companion (`design/characters.json`) &mdash; design only

Age 24. A locksmith who spent a few complicated years opening things she wasn't strictly hired to open, and has mostly gone straight.

## Personality
sly, reformed but not sorry about all of it, meticulous with her hands, trusts a good lock more than a good promise.

## Appearance and dress
Clearly adult, ordinary but gorgeous woman: slim, small-chested with little or no prominent bust, attractive hip/seat silhouette, healthy and active. Charismatic, comfortable in herself and sexually confident. No approved art exists for this draft.

Practical dark clothing with deep pockets for tools, gloves worn thin at the fingertips from delicate work, a set of picks she still carries out of habit.

*Art status: unapproved draft.*

## How you meet her
She's offered a very good, very illegal job at Fort Griffin the same week the outfit needs an honest locksmith, and she'd rather take the honest work if someone makes the case for it.

**To recruit her:**
- Get her a legitimate locksmithing job instead of a lucrative bad one.
- Don't ask her to go back to her old trade unless it's actually necessary.
- Roisin accepts the invitation.

## What she needs
**Raises her strain:** Being tempted back into old, easier, worse habits.

**Recovers it:** Being trusted with something valuable and left alone to prove it was deserved.

## Her adventure: "The Job She Almost Took"
**Objective:** Play as Roisin turning down a lucrative illegal job by completing a legitimate one just as impressive.

**Her skill:** Directly playable precise lockwork under a ticking clock, without it being framed as a heist against anyone sympathetic.

**Your support:** The protagonist runs interference with the old associates trying to pull her back in.

**Payoff:** A legitimate contract, paid in full, and her visible relief at not needing the other kind of job.
