**Status:** romance-eligible companion (`design/characters.json`) &mdash; design only

Age 25. A tanner who cures hides other outfits throw away as ruined.

## Personality
unbothered by smell or mess, practical to a fault, surprisingly gentle humor under a rough trade, values thrift as a virtue, not a shame.

## How you meet her
She offers to cure hides the outfit was ready to discard as ruined, for a fair price and no judgment, and turns out to be better company than the trade's reputation suggests.

**To recruit her:**
- Bring her hides other people considered worthless.
- Don't wrinkle your nose at the work in front of her.
- Philippa accepts the invitation.

## What she needs
**Raises her strain:** Waste -- good material thrown away out of squeamishness or laziness.

**Recovers it:** Someone who doesn't flinch at the mess her honest work makes.
