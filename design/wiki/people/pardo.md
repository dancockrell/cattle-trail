# Pardo

**Status:** minor/supporting character (`design/npcs.json`), category **outfit_hand**

Age 45. The outfit's own senior hand, present since before the room's story starts.

**Location:** Clear Fork

## Personality
steady, few words, the one who actually taught the player the ropes years back.

## Why she/he exists
Background texture for the player's own outfit, distinct from the recruited companions -- someone who was already there, not someone being courted. A source of grounded, unromantic trail wisdom.

## Appearance
Adult man, mid-forties, permanently sun-cured, moves like every motion is considered.

## Voice

> I've buried two bosses. Try not to make it three.

> You ask a lot of questions for a man who already knows the answer.
