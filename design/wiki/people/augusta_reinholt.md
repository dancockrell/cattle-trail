# Augusta Reinholt

**Status:** romance-eligible companion (`design/characters.json`) &mdash; design only

Age 26. An aeronaut experimenting with a small hot-air balloon, half scientist and half showwoman.

## Personality
bold to the point of recklessness, genuinely brilliant about lift and weather, delighted by the sky in a way she can't fully explain, undeterred by past failures.

## Appearance and dress
Clearly adult, ordinary but gorgeous woman: slim, small-chested with little or no prominent bust, attractive hip/seat silhouette, healthy and active. Charismatic, comfortable in herself and sexually confident. No approved art exists for this draft.

Practical flight-appropriate gear, goggles pushed up on her forehead, gloves scorched slightly from burner work.

*Art status: unapproved draft.*

## How you meet her
Her balloon crash-lands near the trail after a test flight goes wrong, and she needs help recovering the damaged envelope and basket before scavengers or weather finish what the crash started.

**To recruit her:**
- Help her recover a damaged balloon after a failed test flight.
- Trust her judgment about weather and lift even when it sounds reckless.
- Augusta accepts the invitation.

## What she needs
**Raises her strain:** A flight failure that could have killed her, dismissed by others as reckless rather than understood as a real risk she calculated.

**Recovers it:** Someone who takes her calculations seriously instead of just her nerve.

## Perk: Sure-Handed Tuning
Reuses the existing shared repair-efficiency perk definition rather than authoring a duplicate.

## Her adventure: "The Flight That Almost Worked"
**Objective:** Play as Augusta repairing her balloon and reading weather conditions carefully enough to actually get airborne again.

**Her skill:** Directly playable weather-reading and repair beats building toward an actual (brief, careful) flight.

**Your support:** The protagonist holds the basket steady and helps read wind conditions from the ground.

**Payoff:** A short, successful flight together, and her open delight at sharing the sky with someone for once.
