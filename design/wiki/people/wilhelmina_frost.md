# Wilhelmina Frost

**Status:** romance-eligible companion (`design/characters.json`) &mdash; design only

Age 23. A sign painter whose lettering hangs above half the storefronts between Griffin and Providence.

## Personality
artistic, surprisingly business-minded about a creative trade, patient with detail work, warm and observant.

## Appearance and dress
Clearly adult, ordinary but gorgeous woman: slim, small-chested with little or no prominent bust, attractive hip/seat silhouette, healthy and active. Charismatic, comfortable in herself and sexually confident. No approved art exists for this draft.

Practical paint-stained work clothes, brushes and pigment jars carried with real care, an artist's attentive eye.

*Art status: unapproved draft.*

## How you meet her
She's partway through a commission when the paint she needs is ruined by weather, and she needs help finding a substitute before her deadline costs her the job and her reputation.

**To recruit her:**
- Bring her a genuinely difficult sign commission.
- Notice and appreciate the craft in her lettering rather than treating it as decoration.
- Wilhelmina accepts the invitation.

## What she needs
**Raises her strain:** Careful work ruined by circumstances outside her control.

**Recovers it:** Someone noticing the craft in work most people walk past without looking up.

## Her adventure: "The Sign That Had to Be Perfect"
**Objective:** Play as Wilhelmina completing a difficult, detailed sign commission under deadline with substitute materials that behave differently than her usual ones.

**Her skill:** Directly playable careful-detail-work-under-adaptation beats.

**Your support:** The protagonist holds the ladder and fetches materials while she works at height.

**Payoff:** A finished sign that draws real admiration, and a small painted token made just for the protagonist.
