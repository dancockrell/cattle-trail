# Perpetua Calder

**Status:** romance-eligible companion (`design/characters.json`) &mdash; design only

Age 27. A fortune teller and card reader whose readings are right often enough that nobody's quite decided if it's skill or something more.

## Personality
theatrical in public, plainspoken in private, genuinely uncertain herself which of her gifts are real, protective of the distinction between comfort and truth.

## Appearance and dress
Clearly adult, ordinary but gorgeous woman: slim, small-chested with little or no prominent bust, attractive hip/seat silhouette, healthy and active. Charismatic, comfortable in herself and sexually confident. No approved art exists for this draft.

Layered, deliberately mysterious traveling clothes for the trade, plainer and more practical underneath when she isn't working.

*Art status: unapproved draft.*

## How you meet her
She's set up a reading table at Split Oak Camp and gives the outfit a reading nobody asked for, that turns out uncomfortably specific.

**To recruit her:**
- Ask for an honest reading instead of a comforting one.
- Don't repeat what she tells you privately as gossip.
- Perpetua accepts the invitation.

## What she needs
**Raises her strain:** Being asked to lie comfortingly instead of read honestly.

**Recovers it:** Someone believing the honest reading over the comforting one.

## Her adventure: "The Reading She Didn't Want to Give"
**Objective:** Play as Perpetua giving a reading she suspects is true and unwelcome, deciding how much of it to actually say out loud.

**Her skill:** Directly playable judgment beats about honesty versus kindness, with real consequences either way.

**Your support:** The protagonist backs whichever choice she makes without second-guessing her in front of the client.

**Payoff:** A private reading just for the protagonist, the first one she's given without charging for it.
