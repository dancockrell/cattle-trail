# Owen Balfour

**Status:** minor/supporting character (`design/npcs.json`), category **rival_outfit**

Age 30. A Hargrove hand who quietly trades off the books when the outfit is short on cash.

**Location:** The Hargrove Range

## Personality
pragmatic, loyal to a ledger, not a flag, friendly in a transactional way, discreet.

## Why she/he exists
Willing to deal cattle or gear off the books when the Hargrove operation is short on cash. Per ECONOMY.md's note that selling directly to a rival outfit is 'typically worse-priced,' he's the exception -- a back-channel the player's outfit could actually use, if the price is right and nobody official hears about it.

## Appearance
Adult man in his early thirties, unremarkable on purpose, an easy smile that doesn't commit to anything.

## Voice
> Reed doesn't need to know every deal I make. Neither does Cass, come to that.

> Cash now beats a better price at Griffin next month. Most days.

> I'm not selling you Hargrove cattle. I'm selling you my time. Difference matters if anyone asks.

## Notes
- Not implemented in scripts/. Gives ECONOMY.md's 'selling directly to a rival outfit' option an actual back-channel actor, since that document currently only names the outfits' principals.
