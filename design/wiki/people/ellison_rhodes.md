# Sergeant Ellison Rhodes

**Status:** minor/supporting character (`design/npcs.json`), category **town**

Age 43. A former cavalry sergeant, now an independent guide working out of Fort Griffin.

**Location:** Fort Griffin

## Personality
disciplined, dry sense of humor, chooses his own jobs now and is particular about it.

## Why she/he exists
A skilled, dignified guide-for-hire distinct from Deacon Marsh's bounty trade -- Rhodes reads country and weather, not people's pasts. A specific, competent person with his own history, not a generic background figure.

## Appearance
Adult man, early forties, upright bearing from years of service, plain but well-kept gear.

## Voice
> I took orders for twenty years. I still will, if the pay and the reason are both good enough.

> I don't guide for outfits that lie to me about what they're actually looking for.

> County like this, the weather kills more men than anything with a name. Watch the sky, not just the trail.
