# Jonas Whitfield

**Status:** minor/supporting character (`design/npcs.json`), category **outfit_hand**

Age 27. The outfit's horse wrangler, manages the remuda separately from the cattle herd.

**Location:** Clear Fork

## Personality
quiet, better with horses than people, particular about gear, trusted with the outfit's whole remuda.

## Why she/he exists
Manages the outfit's spare and saddle horses, a job the whole drive depends on and nobody else wants. Notices before anyone else when a horse is spooked by something that isn't in the manual.

## Appearance
Adult man in his late twenties, lean, rope-burned hands, more at ease near the horse line than the campfire.

## Voice
> Don't crowd her. She'll tell you when she's ready.

> A tired horse under a bad rider gets somebody killed. Rest her.

> Something worked that gray up before you even swung up. Ask me later, not now.
