# Delia Marsh

**Status:** romance-eligible companion (`design/characters.json`) &mdash; design only

Age 25. Rides fence and night-guards water for the Hargrove Range, close enough to the family's books to know the new spread is mortgaged past what its half-built barn shows; she's stayed loyal out of pay owed, not conviction, and watches Clear Fork's outfit for a fairer offer.

## Personality
guarded, dryly observant, fiercely fair, slow to trust, decisive once committed.

## Appearance and dress
Clearly adult, ordinary but gorgeous woman: slim, small-chested with little or no prominent bust, attractive hip/seat silhouette, healthy and active. Charismatic, comfortable in herself and sexually confident. No approved art exists for this draft.

A cast-off Hargrove foreman's coat let out to fit her, a plain rifle scabbard she keeps oiled better than the coat, boots resoled twice, hair kept short and practical under a flat hat.

*Art status: unapproved draft.*

## How you meet her
She's mending a stretch of Hargrove fence exactly on the property line when the outfit catches her looking toward Clear Fork instead of at her work.

**To recruit her:**
- Prove the outfit deals fairer than Hargrove does, in front of her.
- Don't ask her to badmouth the family that still signs her pay, only to leave them.
- Delia accepts the invitation.

## What she needs
**Raises her strain:** Being asked to choose a side out loud, in front of people who'll remember it.

**Recovers it:** Being told plainly where she stands with someone, and left to decide the rest herself.

## Perk: Steady Aim
Reuses the existing shot-range perk definition; years of riding fence alone taught her to see trouble before it's close.

## Her adventure: "Past the Property Line"
**Objective:** Play as Delia moving her own string of Hargrove-branded horses across the property line by night without a confrontation that would burn the bridge for good.

**Her skill:** Directly playable quiet night riding and route choice under real discovery risk, not a stealth-meter abstraction.

**Your support:** The protagonist waits at the line to vouch for her the moment she crosses, so the choice reads as hers and not a rescue.

**Payoff:** She hands over the reins on Clear Fork ground for the first time and doesn't look back toward Hargrove once.
