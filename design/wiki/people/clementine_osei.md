stoic, efficient under pressure, dark humor, protective without being smothering.

## How you meet her
She's treating a wounded traveler at a Split Oak Camp fire when the outfit rides in mid-crisis and either helps or gets in the way.

**To recruit her:**
- Handle an actual medical emergency competently in front of her.
- Don't panic when she needs a second pair of steady hands.
- Clementine accepts the invitation.

## What she needs
**Raises her strain:** Watching someone die from a wound that competent, faster care could have stopped.

**Recovers it:** Practical company, not sympathy -- she'll ask for help before she asks for comfort.

## Her adventure: "The Wound That Won't Wait"
**Objective:** Play as Clementine treating a serious injury against the clock with limited supplies.

**Her skill:** Directly playable field-medicine triage beats: prioritize, improvise, stay calm.

**Your support:** The protagonist fetches exactly what she asks for, in the order she asks for it.

**Payoff:** The patient stabilizes, and she finally sits down and lets her own hands shake.
