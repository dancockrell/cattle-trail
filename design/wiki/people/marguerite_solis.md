# Marguerite Solis

**Status:** romance-eligible companion (`design/characters.json`) &mdash; design only

Age 26. A midwife and folk healer who trusts what she can see and touch over what anyone tells her secondhand.

## Personality
unflappable, blunt, deeply competent, warm in a way that shows up in actions not words.

## Appearance and dress
Clearly adult, ordinary but gorgeous woman: slim, small-chested with little or no prominent bust, attractive hip/seat silhouette, healthy and active. Charismatic, comfortable in herself and sexually confident. No approved art exists for this draft.

Practical dark dress, sleeves that roll up fast, a bag of instruments and herbs she's particular about, hair kept short and out of the way.

*Art status: unapproved draft.*

## How you meet her
A difficult birth at a homestead near the trail needs a competent hand fast, and hers is the only one for miles.

**To recruit her:**
- Get her to a birth or a bad injury in time.
- Follow her instructions without arguing mid-crisis.
- Marguerite accepts the invitation.

## What she needs
**Raises her strain:** Losing someone she was sure she could save.

**Recovers it:** Being useful, not comforted.

## Perk: Good Company
Reuses the existing shared camp-company perk definition rather than authoring a duplicate.

## Her adventure: "The Night It Went Wrong"
**Objective:** Play as Marguerite managing a genuinely difficult birth with calm, specific instructions rather than panic.

**Her skill:** Directly playable calm-under-pressure decision beats, not a horror scene.

**Your support:** The protagonist runs for water, light and a second pair of hands exactly when told to.

**Payoff:** A newborn's first cry, and Marguerite finally sitting down.

## Notes
- Name, appearance and encounter are provisional. Written carefully to avoid horror-genre treatment of childbirth: the tension is competence under pressure, and the outcome is not left ambiguous or fatal.
- Not implemented in scripts/. This is a design-only record awaiting a room.
