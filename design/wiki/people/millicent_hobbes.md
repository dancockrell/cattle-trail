# Millicent Hobbes

**Status:** romance-eligible companion (`design/characters.json`) &mdash; design only

Age 26. An upholsterer who makes hard travel seats and saddle-adjacent gear genuinely comfortable.

## Personality
practical, surprisingly particular about padding and fit, warm, unhurried manner, takes real comfort seriously as a craft.

## Appearance and dress
Clearly adult, ordinary but gorgeous woman: slim, small-chested with little or no prominent bust, attractive hip/seat silhouette, healthy and active. Charismatic, comfortable in herself and sexually confident. No approved art exists for this draft.

Practical work clothes, a padded apron, tools for cutting and stuffing fabric, patient careful hands.

*Art status: unapproved draft.*

## How you meet her
The outfit's wagon seat has become genuinely unbearable on long days and she offers to rebuild it properly rather than patch it with more rags.

**To recruit her:**
- Bring her a genuinely uncomfortable wagon seat or saddle worth fixing.
- Trust her judgment about padding and support.
- Millicent accepts the invitation.

## What she needs
**Raises her strain:** Comfort dismissed as unimportant when it's actually what keeps people functional on a long trail.

**Recovers it:** Being taken seriously about something most people consider trivial.

## Perk: Sure-Handed Tuning
Reuses the existing shared repair-efficiency perk definition rather than authoring a duplicate.

## Her adventure: "The Seat That Finally Fit"
**Objective:** Play as Millicent rebuilding a wagon seat's padding and structure to actually fit the person using it, not a generic template.

**Her skill:** Directly playable fit-and-comfort craft beats, a genuinely different problem from structural repair.

**Your support:** The protagonist tests each iteration honestly rather than politely approving early.

**Payoff:** A seat that's actually comfortable, and her satisfaction at solving a problem everyone else dismissed.
