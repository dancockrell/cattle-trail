# Tam

**Status:** minor/supporting character (`design/npcs.json`), category **wanderer**

Age not stated. A spirit-touched boy who runs messages between camps and outfits.

**Location:** unplaced

## Personality
quiet, sees things adults argue about, unbothered by the dead.

## Why she/he exists
A living child character used carefully: not a companion, not in danger for shock value, a message-carrier who treats the ordinary supernatural of this world exactly as ordinary as LORE.md says it is. His age is deliberately unspecified rather than stated, since he is not a romance-adjacent character and does not need one.

## Appearance
A child, practical patched clothes, unhurried in a way that unsettles adults more than it should.

## Voice
> The lady at the crossing says to tell you she's still there. She always is, though.

> Grown-ups ask if I'm scared. I don't know what I'd be scared of that isn't also just Tuesday.

> I can carry a message further than a rumor. Rumors get things wrong on the way.
