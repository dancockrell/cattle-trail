# Sable Quinn

**Status:** romance-eligible companion (`design/characters.json`) &mdash; design only

Age 24. Broke and re-shod horses for a West Texas remount outfit until debt collectors took the herd out from under her family; now she hires her hands and her eye for a green horse out to whichever outfit can use a wrangler who won't lose a string on a bad crossing.

## Personality
unflappable, competitive, wry, protective of horses before people.

## Appearance and dress
Clearly adult, ordinary but gorgeous woman: slim, small-chested with little or no prominent bust, attractive hip/seat silhouette, healthy and active. Charismatic, comfortable in herself and sexually confident. No approved art exists for this draft.

Split canvas trousers under a long duster for brush country, rawhide chaps scarred from mesquite, a battered flat-brim hat tied down against the wind, sleeves rolled to the forearm.

*Art status: unapproved draft.*

## How you meet her
She's talking down a spooked gelding at the edge of Clear Fork's remuda line when a rope snaps loose and the whole string nearly scatters.

**To recruit her:**
- Help her catch and settle the scattered remuda before a horse is lost or hurt.
- Let her handle the gelding her own way instead of forcing it.
- Sable accepts the invitation.

## What she needs
**Raises her strain:** A good horse ruined by somebody else's impatience.

**Recovers it:** Time at the horse line before anything else, then quiet.

## Perk: Trail Hand
Reuses the existing lasso/lead-duration perk definition; her hand on the rope holds a spooked string together longer.

## Her adventure: "The Scattered Remuda"
**Objective:** Play as Sable running down and re-gathering a spooked, scattered horse string across open ground before dark, without losing a single horse to a bad step.

**Her skill:** Directly playable mounted herding and rope work under time pressure, not a menu.

**Your support:** The protagonist rides drag, turning strays back toward her instead of chasing them down herself.

**Payoff:** She swings down beside the last horse caught, breathless and grinning, and admits she's glad the protagonist rode drag instead of getting in her way.
