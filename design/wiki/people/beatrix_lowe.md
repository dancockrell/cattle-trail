# Beatrix Lowe

**Status:** romance-eligible companion (`design/characters.json`) &mdash; design only

Age 25. A cartographer and surveyor mapping county lines nobody agrees on yet.

## Personality
precise, delights in an accurate map more than most things, diplomatic under pressure, surprisingly fearless about difficult terrain.

## Appearance and dress
Clearly adult, ordinary but gorgeous woman: slim, small-chested with little or no prominent bust, attractive hip/seat silhouette, healthy and active. Charismatic, comfortable in herself and sexually confident. No approved art exists for this draft.

Practical field clothes, a satchel of instruments and rolled charts, ink-stained fingers, a compass worn like jewelry.

*Art status: unapproved draft.*

## How you meet her
She's caught between the Calloway and Hargrove outfits' competing claims about where the water rights actually run, and needs an escort who won't take a side before the numbers are in.

**To recruit her:**
- Help her resurvey a disputed boundary line.
- Take her measurements seriously even when a rancher doesn't.
- Beatrix accepts the invitation.

## What she needs
**Raises her strain:** A map being ignored because the truth in it was inconvenient.

**Recovers it:** Being trusted with the actual numbers before anyone argues about them.

## Her adventure: "The Line Both Outfits Want"
**Objective:** Play as Beatrix resurveying a disputed boundary under pressure from both the Calloways and the Hargroves to get a favorable result.

**Her skill:** Directly playable precise measurement beats: the correct answer exists and cannot be flattered into changing.

**Your support:** The protagonist holds the chain and keeps both outfits' riders at a respectful distance.

**Payoff:** A finished, honest map, and her quiet satisfaction that neither side got to argue with the truth.
