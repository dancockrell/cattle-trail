# Sunniva Larsen

**Status:** romance-eligible companion (`design/characters.json`) &mdash; design only

Age 21. A confectioner whose traveling candy cart is the closest thing to luxury most of the county sees.

## Personality
bright, generous to a fault, hides real business shrewdness behind a sweet manner, delights in small pleasures.

## Appearance and dress
Clearly adult, ordinary but gorgeous woman: slim, small-chested with little or no prominent bust, attractive hip/seat silhouette, healthy and active. Charismatic, comfortable in herself and sexually confident. No approved art exists for this draft.

A cheerful practical dress suited to cart work, an apron dusted with sugar, a cart of jars and tins she keeps meticulously organized.

*Art status: unapproved draft.*

## How you meet her
Her cart's wheel breaks on a bad stretch of road with a storm coming, threatening to melt and ruin a season's worth of careful stock.

**To recruit her:**
- Help her protect her cart and stock from a real threat.
- Actually enjoy something she makes rather than politely declining.
- Sunniva accepts the invitation.

## What she needs
**Raises her strain:** Watching something she made with real care go to waste.

**Recovers it:** Someone enjoying what she makes without needing convincing.

## Her adventure: "The Cart That Couldn't Wait"
**Objective:** Play as Sunniva racing to get her cart under cover and her stock saved before a storm ruins it.

**Her skill:** Directly playable triage-under-weather beats, lighter in tone than Cordelia's parallel book-wagon crisis.

**Your support:** The protagonist hauls the heavy jars while she manages the delicate stock.

**Payoff:** Everything saved, and a treat handed over that she made specifically with the protagonist's taste in mind.
