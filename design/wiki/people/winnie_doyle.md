# Winnifred "Winnie" Doyle

**Status:** romance-eligible companion (`design/characters.json`) &mdash; design only

Age 23. A traveling seamstress and dressmaker who reads a county's fortunes by what people can afford to wear.

## Personality
observant, quietly funny, particular about her work, kinder than her first impression.

## How you meet her
She's mending trail-torn gear at a Providence stall when a piece of the outfit's own kit needs her needle.

**To recruit her:**
- Bring her a torn coat or dress that matters to someone.
- Notice the mending is better than the original.
- Winnie accepts the invitation.

## What she needs
**Raises her strain:** Watching good cloth ruined by carelessness.

**Recovers it:** Something to mend, and quiet while she does it.

## Her adventure: "The Coat That Mattered"
**Objective:** Play as Winnie repairing a keepsake coat well enough to be worn again, under a deadline that matters more than the stitching alone.

**Her skill:** Directly playable careful needlework under time pressure, not a menu.

**Your support:** The protagonist holds the lamp and keeps the story of why the coat matters going while she works.

**Payoff:** She holds it up finished, and it's clearly better than new.
