# Zenobia Whitmore

**Status:** romance-eligible companion (`design/characters.json`) &mdash; design only

Age 23. A prospector and self-taught geologist working a fresh claim, distinct from Bellhollow's played-out ground.

## Personality
stubbornly optimistic, genuinely knowledgeable about rock and ore, wary of claim-jumpers, warm once trust is earned.

## Appearance and dress
Clearly adult, ordinary but gorgeous woman: slim, small-chested with little or no prominent bust, attractive hip/seat silhouette, healthy and active. Charismatic, comfortable in herself and sexually confident. No approved art exists for this draft.

Practical field clothes built for climbing and digging, a rock hammer at her belt, sample bags full of carefully labeled ore.

*Art status: unapproved draft.*

## How you meet her
Someone is trying to jump her legally-filed claim while she's away resupplying, and she needs backup to hold it until the paperwork is settled.

**To recruit her:**
- Help her defend a legitimate claim from a jumper.
- Take her geological read of the ground seriously.
- Zenobia accepts the invitation.

## What she needs
**Raises her strain:** Honest work stolen by someone with a faster gun and no legal claim.

**Recovers it:** Someone believing her read of the ground without needing it proven twice.

## Perk: Between the Footprints
Reuses the existing shared trail-hazard perk definition rather than authoring a duplicate -- a prospector's eye for the ground works the same as a scout's.

## Her adventure: "The Claim Someone Else Wants"
**Objective:** Play as Zenobia holding a legitimate claim against a jumper using paperwork and presence rather than a gunfight.

**Her skill:** Directly playable legal-and-practical defense beats, de-escalation preferred over violence.

**Your support:** The protagonist backs her claim visibly without escalating first.

**Payoff:** The claim holds, on paper and on the ground, and she finally shows what she thinks is actually down there.
