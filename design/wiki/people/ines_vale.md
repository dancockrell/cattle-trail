## How you meet her
Meet Ines on the northern trail after Ada's cart outing is complete.

**To recruit her:**
- Read the southern trail grass, northern trail stones and southeast scrub clues.
- Return to Ines with all three clues to complete the trail.
- Ines offers to scout for the outfit; accept her invitation.

## What she needs

**Recovers it:** An embrace or quiet time with a trusted loved one.
