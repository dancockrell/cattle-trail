# Granny Combs

**Status:** minor/supporting character (`design/npcs.json`), category **family**

Age 74. Loretta Combs's grandmother, who taught her to quilt and started the county-memory tradition.

**Location:** unplaced

## Personality
frail but sharp, the actual origin of Loretta's project, carries the oldest stories in the county.

## Why she/he exists
Gives Loretta Combs's quilt-memory project a real origin and a source of the county's oldest, least-recorded history.

## Appearance
Adult woman, mid-seventies, frail but alert, surrounded by fabric and the visible evidence of a lifetime's work.

## Voice
> I started stitching what I saw so it wouldn't just disappear when I did. Loretta just does it better than I ever did.

> Ask me about the county forty years back. Ask me before you can't anymore.

> My hands don't hold a needle steady these days. Hers do. That's how it should work.
