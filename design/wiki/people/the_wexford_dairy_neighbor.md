# Hosea Pruett

**Status:** minor/supporting character (`design/npcs.json`), category **antagonist**

Age 50. A neighboring rancher who resents Briony Wexford's water usage for her dairy herd.

**Location:** unplaced

## Personality
stubborn, genuinely believes he's the wronged party, not violent, just petty and persistent.

## Why she/he exists
A small-scale, human-sized antagonist for Briony Wexford distinct from the county's larger rival-outfit or supernatural threats -- ordinary neighborly friction over water.

## Appearance
Adult man, fifty, ordinary rancher, arms crossed at a fence line, aggrieved rather than dangerous posture.

## Voice
> Her cows drink what my cattle need. Nobody asked me if that arrangement was fair.

> I've complained to three different people about her water use. None of them have done anything about it either.

> I don't want a feud. I want an answer. I haven't gotten one, so here we are.
