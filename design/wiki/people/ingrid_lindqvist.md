# Ingrid Lindqvist

**Status:** romance-eligible companion (`design/characters.json`) &mdash; design only

Age 24. A weaver and dyer whose colors hold where everyone else's fade.

## Personality
particular about color, patient with slow processes, quietly stubborn, finds satisfaction in getting a shade exactly right.

## Appearance and dress
Clearly adult, ordinary but gorgeous woman: slim, small-chested with little or no prominent bust, attractive hip/seat silhouette, healthy and active. Charismatic, comfortable in herself and sexually confident. No approved art exists for this draft.

A dye-stained practical dress, sleeves permanently discolored at the cuffs, a satchel of pigments and mordants she guards closely.

*Art status: unapproved draft.*

## How you meet her
She needs a specific plant that only grows in a hard-to-reach stretch of the timber and plains country, and the outfit's route happens to pass near it.

**To recruit her:**
- Help her source a rare dyestuff before a competitor does.
- Appreciate the actual difference in her colors instead of assuming cloth is cloth.
- Ingrid accepts the invitation.

## What she needs
**Raises her strain:** A dye lot ruined by impatience, hers or someone else's.

**Recovers it:** Quiet company during a long, exacting process.

## Her adventure: "The Color That Won't Hold"
**Objective:** Play as Ingrid gathering a rare dye plant and running a delicate, timing-sensitive dye process before it spoils.

**Her skill:** Directly playable careful-timing beats around a real craft process, not abstracted into a menu.

**Your support:** The protagonist keeps watch for anyone else after the same plant.

**Payoff:** A finished bolt of cloth in a color nobody else in the county can make, given to the protagonist first.
