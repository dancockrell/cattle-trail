**Status:** romance-eligible companion (`design/characters.json`) &mdash; design only

Age 22. A basket weaver whose work is prized for holding weight nobody expects woven reed to hold.

## How you meet her
The outfit needs a way to carry an awkward, heavy load across rough country and she offers to weave something purpose-built rather than sell them something generic.

**To recruit her:**
- Bring her a genuinely difficult load-bearing basket to weave.
- Trust her weave over a store-bought container.
- Phoebe accepts the invitation.

## What she needs
**Raises her strain:** A woven piece failing under a load it should have held.

**Recovers it:** Quiet, repetitive shared work rather than conversation.
