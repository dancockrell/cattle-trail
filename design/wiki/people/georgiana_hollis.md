## How you meet her
A rival is trying to poach her recipe or undercut her supply chain, and she'd rather handle it with the outfit's backing than alone, though she'd manage either way.

**To recruit her:**
- Help her defend her recipe or her supply from someone trying to steal either.
- Respect that her experience earns her the confidence she carries.
- Georgiana accepts the invitation.

## What she needs
**Raises her strain:** Being underestimated because of her age or her trade.

**Recovers it:** Being treated as an equal, not a mentor figure by default.
