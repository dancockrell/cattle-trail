# Rosamund Achterberg

**Status:** romance-eligible companion (`design/characters.json`) &mdash; design only

Age 24. An herbalist who tends an actual garden rather than working from a satchel of dried supplies, distinct from Marguerite's clinical midwifery and Dinah's laboratory chemistry.

## Personality
gentle, deeply patient with growing things, quietly stubborn about doing things the slow, right way, warm and grounded.

## Appearance and dress
Clearly adult, ordinary but gorgeous woman: slim, small-chested with little or no prominent bust, attractive hip/seat silhouette, healthy and active. Charismatic, comfortable in herself and sexually confident. No approved art exists for this draft.

A practical gardening dress, soil-marked hands, a wide-brimmed hat, baskets of cuttings and seedlings.

*Art status: unapproved draft.*

## How you meet her
A hard frost threatens to destroy her carefully cultivated medicinal garden right before harvest, and she needs help protecting it through one dangerous night.

**To recruit her:**
- Help her protect her garden from a real threat -- weather, animals, or a land dispute.
- Trust a slow remedy over a fast one when she says it matters.
- Rosamund accepts the invitation.

## What she needs
**Raises her strain:** A season's careful cultivation destroyed by one bad night she couldn't prevent.

**Recovers it:** Slow, patient company tending something growing.

## Her adventure: "The Frost That Almost Took Everything"
**Objective:** Play as Rosamund protecting a vulnerable garden through a genuinely dangerous frost night using practical, unglamorous methods.

**Her skill:** Directly playable resource-triage-under-weather beats, a quieter, slower-paced parallel to Sunniva's and Cordelia's storm crises.

**Your support:** The protagonist hauls covers and water through the cold while she directs the effort.

**Payoff:** The garden saved, and cuttings from it given to the protagonist to grow anywhere they end up.
