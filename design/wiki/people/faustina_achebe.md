# Faustina Achebe

**Status:** romance-eligible companion (`design/characters.json`) &mdash; design only

Age 23. A beekeeper and honey trader whose hives survive conditions that kill everyone else's.

## Personality
unhurried, finds most human drama less interesting than her bees, quietly brave around anything that stings, generous with what she makes.

## Appearance and dress
Clearly adult, ordinary but gorgeous woman: slim, small-chested with little or no prominent bust, attractive hip/seat silhouette, healthy and active. Charismatic, comfortable in herself and sexually confident. No approved art exists for this draft.

A practical veiled hat and light protective coat for hive work, otherwise plain trail clothes, honey-gold trim she added herself.

## How you meet her
A storm scattered her hives near the trail and she needs help recovering them before the colonies die or scatter for good.

**To recruit her:**
- Help her recover a hive knocked over in a storm.
- Don't swat at anything near her without asking first.
- Faustina accepts the invitation.

## What she needs
**Raises her strain:** A hive lost to carelessness that could have been prevented.

**Recovers it:** Slow, unhurried company -- her bees taught her patience and she expects the same back.

## Her adventure: "The Scattered Hives"
**Objective:** Play as Faustina calmly recovering and re-housing scattered bee colonies without panicking anyone, human or otherwise.

**Her skill:** Directly playable calm, deliberate handling under a mildly stressful but non-violent threat.

**Your support:** The protagonist keeps a respectful distance and passes her tools on request.

**Payoff:** A jar of the season's first honey, offered rather than sold.
