# Esme Radcliffe

**Status:** minor/supporting character (`design/npcs.json`), category **rival_outfit**

Age 26. A Hargrove line rider who patrols the disputed water boundary.

**Location:** The Hargrove Range

## Personality
tense, professionally polite even when she'd rather not be, takes the boundary dispute personally, sharp-eyed.

## Why she/he exists
Rides the fence line where the Hargrove-Calloway water dispute actually plays out on the ground -- the hand the player's outfit would cross paths with first if a drive strayed near that stretch, before it ever reaches Reed or Cass directly.

## Appearance
Adult woman in her mid-twenties, alert posture, a rifle scabbarded but present.

## Voice
> That water's contested. Doesn't mean it's yours to cross whistling.

> I don't make the boundary. I just get blamed when somebody ignores it.

> Ask the lawyers where the line is. I just ride it.
