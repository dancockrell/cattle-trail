# Dorcas Pym

**Status:** romance-eligible companion (`design/characters.json`) &mdash; design only

Age 20. A rat-catcher and pest-control specialist whose small, unglamorous trade keeps granaries and stores across the county functional.

## Personality
unbothered by vermin or mess, surprisingly cheerful, takes real pride in an overlooked trade, quick and clever.

## Appearance and dress
Clearly adult, ordinary but gorgeous woman: slim, small-chested with little or no prominent bust, attractive hip/seat silhouette, healthy and active. Charismatic, comfortable in herself and sexually confident. No approved art exists for this draft.

Practical, sturdy work clothes suited to crawling into tight unpleasant spaces, a satchel of traps and tools, a small trained terrier at her heel.

*Art status: unapproved draft.*

## How you meet her
The outfit's grain stores are being destroyed by an infestation nobody else has managed to solve, and she offers to fix it properly rather than have them lose the whole supply.

**To recruit her:**
- Take her trade seriously instead of laughing it off.
- Help her deal with an infestation actually threatening the outfit's stores.
- Dorcas accepts the invitation.

## What she needs
**Raises her strain:** Being laughed at for a trade that quietly keeps people fed.

**Recovers it:** Someone taking her work seriously instead of treating it as a punchline.

## Perk: Between the Footprints
Reuses the existing shared trail-hazard perk definition rather than authoring a duplicate -- she notices small signs of trouble nobody else looks for.

## Her adventure: "The Infestation Nobody Else Could Fix"
**Objective:** Play as Dorcas solving a genuinely difficult pest infestation through cleverness and patience rather than brute force.

**Her skill:** Directly playable small-scale-problem-solving beats with a lighter, more comic tone than most of the roster's adventures.

**Your support:** The protagonist blocks escape routes and holds supplies while she works.

**Payoff:** The stores saved, and her small trained terrier deciding it likes the protagonist specifically.
