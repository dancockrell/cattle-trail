# Odalys Bright

**Status:** romance-eligible companion (`design/characters.json`) &mdash; design only

Age 22. A trick rider and exhibition performer between shows, restless without an audience.

## Personality
showy, genuinely skilled beneath the flash, craves motion, surprisingly thoughtful once the performance stops.

## Appearance and dress
Clearly adult, ordinary but gorgeous woman: slim, small-chested with little or no prominent bust, attractive hip/seat silhouette, healthy and active. Charismatic, comfortable in herself and sexually confident. No approved art exists for this draft.

A flashier riding outfit than most, fringe and bright color, built for show as much as function, scuffed from real practice.

*Art status: unapproved draft.*

## How you meet her
Her exhibition troupe folded between towns and she's been doing trick-riding demonstrations for tips, looking for a reason to ride that isn't a paying crowd.

**To recruit her:**
- Watch her actually perform and mean the applause.
- Give her something harder than a show to prove herself against.
- Odalys accepts the invitation.

## What she needs
**Raises her strain:** Performing for a crowd that claps but doesn't actually see her.

**Recovers it:** An audience of one who actually watches.
