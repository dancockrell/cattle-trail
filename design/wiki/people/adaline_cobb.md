## How you meet her
The outfit's boots are failing catastrophically mid-trail and she offers to rebuild them properly on the spot rather than sell a quick, worse replacement.

**To recruit her:**
- Bring her boots genuinely worth rebuilding rather than replacing.
- Trust her fit judgment over a cheaper off-the-shelf pair.
- Adaline accepts the invitation.

## What she needs
**Raises her strain:** Someone suffering an avoidable injury because of neglected, badly-fitted footwear.

**Recovers it:** Physical work alongside someone rather than being handed a finished pair.

## Her adventure: "The Boots That Had to Last"
**Objective:** Play as Adaline rebuilding a genuinely failing pair of boots correctly under time pressure, refusing a faster worse fix.

**Her skill:** Directly playable careful-fit-and-craft beats, distinct in scale and feel from Petra's saddlery and Marcella's wagon work.

**Your support:** The protagonist holds materials steady and learns the trade a little by watching closely.
