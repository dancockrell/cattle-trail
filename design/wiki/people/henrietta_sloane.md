# Henrietta Sloane

**Status:** romance-eligible companion (`design/characters.json`) &mdash; design only

Age 24. A milliner whose hats are the one indulgence half the county allows itself.

## Personality
particular about proportion and color, surprisingly sharp businesswoman, warm with regular customers, unbothered by being underestimated as 'just a hat lady'.

## Appearance and dress
Clearly adult, ordinary but gorgeous woman: slim, small-chested with little or no prominent bust, attractive hip/seat silhouette, healthy and active. Charismatic, comfortable in herself and sexually confident. No approved art exists for this draft.

A well-made (of course) hat, practical dress suited to close needlework, a workbasket of trims and blocks she guards closely.

*Art status: unapproved draft.*

## How you meet her
A shipment of imported trims she can't easily replace was stolen off a freight wagon, and she needs help tracking it down before her whole season's stock is ruined.

**To recruit her:**
- Recover a shipment of trims lost or stolen in transit.
- Take her craft seriously instead of dismissing it as frivolous.
- Henrietta accepts the invitation.

## What she needs
**Raises her strain:** Being dismissed as frivolous for a trade that actually keeps her independent.

**Recovers it:** Being asked her opinion on something and having it taken seriously.

## Her adventure: "The Trims That Went Missing"
**Objective:** Play as Henrietta tracking down stolen trade goods through legitimate business channels and a little social pressure, not a gunfight.

**Her skill:** Directly playable investigation-through-commerce beats: who sold what to whom, and when.

**Your support:** The protagonist provides the physical presence while she does the actual detective work.

**Payoff:** The recovered shipment, and a genuinely excellent hat made specifically for the protagonist as thanks.
