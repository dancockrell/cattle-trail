## Personality
methodical, takes real pride in joinery done right, unbothered by being covered in sawdust, warm once a shared project starts.

## How you meet her
The outfit needs a proper supply chest built to survive the trail rather than a store-bought one that won't, and she's the only carpenter who'll build it right instead of fast.

**To recruit her:**
- Bring her a genuinely difficult piece to build or repair.
- Respect that good furniture takes the time it takes.
- Wilhelmina accepts the invitation.

## What she needs
**Raises her strain:** Good joinery undone by someone rushing it or forcing it.

**Recovers it:** Physical work alongside someone rather than being handed a finished thing.
