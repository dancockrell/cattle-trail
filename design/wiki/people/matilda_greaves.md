# Matilda Greaves

**Status:** romance-eligible companion (`design/characters.json`) &mdash; design only

Age 23. A cartographer's rival in method: a self-taught mapmaker who works from lived trail experience rather than Beatrix Lowe's formal surveying instruments.

## Personality
intuitive, distrustful of instruments she can't personally verify, warm and practical, surprisingly accurate despite unconventional methods.

## Appearance and dress
Clearly adult, ordinary but gorgeous woman: slim, small-chested with little or no prominent bust, attractive hip/seat silhouette, healthy and active. Charismatic, comfortable in herself and sexually confident. No approved art exists for this draft.

Practical trail clothes, a satchel of hand-drawn maps annotated in her own shorthand, calloused capable hands from years of riding while sketching.

*Art status: unapproved draft.*

## How you meet her
Beatrix Lowe's formal survey and Matilda's own hand-drawn map disagree about a stretch of trail, and someone needs to actually ride it to find out which one is right before an outfit gets lost trusting the wrong version.

**To recruit her:**
- Trust her hand-drawn map over a more official but less current one.
- Help her verify and update a map before an outfit relies on a wrong one.
- Matilda accepts the invitation.

## What she needs
**Raises her strain:** A map trusted over lived experience, leading someone astray.

**Recovers it:** Being trusted for what she's actually seen, not just what's officially recorded.

## Perk: Between the Footprints
Reuses the existing shared trail-hazard perk definition rather than authoring a duplicate -- lived trail knowledge reads hazards as well as formal surveying does.

## Her adventure: "The Two Maps That Disagreed"
**Objective:** Play as Matilda riding a disputed stretch of trail to determine whose map is actually correct, resolving a disagreement with evidence rather than authority.

**Her skill:** Directly playable trail-verification beats that pair naturally with Beatrix Lowe's surveying adventure without duplicating it.

**Your support:** The protagonist keeps track of landmarks while she cross-checks against both maps.

**Payoff:** A corrected map that both mapmakers actually agree on afterward, and mutual respect instead of rivalry.
