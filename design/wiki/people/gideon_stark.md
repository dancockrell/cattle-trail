# Gideon Stark

**Status:** minor/supporting character (`design/npcs.json`), category **town**

Age 63. One of the last holdouts at Bellhollow, refuses to leave the played-out mining camp.

**Location:** Bellhollow

## Personality
stubborn past reason, not senile, just committed, knows more about the bell than he'll say plainly.

## Why she/he exists
A living source of information about the Bellhollow haunting who won't give a straight answer, per LORE.md's instruction not to write a monster manual -- his evasions are the content, not a lore dump.

## Appearance
Adult man, early sixties, gaunt from a hard decade, clothes patched rather than replaced.

## Voice
> Bell rings when it rings. I stopped asking it to explain itself years back.

> This claim paid out once. I'm not fool enough to think it owes me twice, but I'm staying anyway.

> You want the story straight, go ask the bell. It's got as much to say as I do on a bad night.
