**Status:** romance-eligible companion (`design/characters.json`) &mdash; design only

Age 26. A deputized peacekeeper working the county's edges where Sheriff Dell's authority formally ends.

## Personality
formal, fair to a fault, carries her badge like a promise rather than a threat, dry about the irony of her name.

## Appearance and dress
Clearly adult, ordinary but gorgeous woman: slim, small-chested with little or no prominent bust, attractive hip/seat silhouette, healthy and active. Charismatic, comfortable in herself and sexually confident. No approved art exists for this draft.

## How you meet her
Her jurisdiction runs out exactly where the outfit's trouble starts, and she's decided to help anyway rather than watch it happen just past her authority.

**To recruit her:**
- Help her make a lawful arrest without it turning violent.
- Respect the line she draws between the law and a favor.
- Temperance accepts the invitation.

## What she needs
**Raises her strain:** Watching the law fail someone it was supposed to protect.

**Recovers it:** Being trusted to be off duty without stopping being herself.

**Objective:** Play as Temperance making a formal arrest that has to hold up later, not just win the moment.

**Her skill:** Directly playable procedural-correctness beats: read the situation, act within the law, document it.

**Your support:** The protagonist backs her play without taking the badge's authority from her.

**Payoff:** Paperwork done right, and an off-duty drink she actually lets herself enjoy.
