# Concepcion Ibarra

**Status:** romance-eligible companion (`design/characters.json`) &mdash; design only

Age 25. A freighter and teamster who has driven harder roads than this trail and rates every wagon against them.

## Personality
blunt, unshakeable under pressure, loyal to her rigs and her people equally, surprisingly gentle with animals.

## Appearance and dress
Clearly adult, ordinary but gorgeous woman: slim, small-chested with little or no prominent bust, attractive hip/seat silhouette, healthy and active. Charismatic, comfortable in herself and sexually confident. No approved art exists for this draft.

Heavy driving coat, gloves built for reins and rope, boots made for standing on a moving wagon box, hair braided tight and out of the way.

*Art status: unapproved draft.*

## How you meet her
Her own freight outfit lost a wagon to a bad crossing and she's short a team and a driver's second pair of hands to finish the run.

**To recruit her:**
- Help her get a heavy wagon through a bad stretch of road.
- Trust her judgment about the team over your own.
- Concepcion accepts the invitation.

## What she needs
**Raises her strain:** A team pushed past what they could safely give.

**Recovers it:** Physical work alongside someone rather than being minded.

## Perk: Good Company
Reuses the existing shared camp-company perk definition rather than authoring a duplicate -- the closest existing fit for the teamster family archetype, since no freight-specific stat exists in the runtime yet.

## Her adventure: "The Grade That Takes Wagons"
**Objective:** Play as Concepcion driving a loaded wagon down a genuinely difficult grade, managing brake, team and load together.

**Her skill:** Directly playable driving-under-load beats: pace, brake timing, reading the team's strain.

**Your support:** The protagonist works the brake block on the protagonist's own signal from her.

**Payoff:** The wagon levels out at the bottom intact, and she finally lets her shoulders down.
