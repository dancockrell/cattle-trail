# Artemisia Falk

**Status:** romance-eligible companion (`design/characters.json`) &mdash; design only

Age 24. A falconer whose trained birds do work most people assume needs a rifle instead.

## Personality
quiet, deeply patient, communicates as much through her birds' behavior as her own words, fiercely protective of her hawks.

## Appearance and dress
Clearly adult, ordinary but gorgeous woman: slim, small-chested with little or no prominent bust, attractive hip/seat silhouette, healthy and active. Charismatic, comfortable in herself and sexually confident. No approved art exists for this draft.

Practical leather gear built for handling birds of prey, a hooded hawk often on her arm, calm deliberate movements.

*Art status: unapproved draft.*

## How you meet her
One of her trained hawks goes missing during a storm and she needs help tracking it before it's lost for good or hurts someone unfamiliar with handling it.

**To recruit her:**
- Trust her hawk's read of the situation over your own assumption.
- Never startle or threaten her birds.
- Artemisia accepts the invitation.

## What she needs
**Raises her strain:** A trained bird lost or harmed because someone didn't respect what it was.

**Recovers it:** Quiet company that doesn't need to fill every silence.

## Perk: Between the Footprints
Reuses the existing shared trail-hazard perk definition rather than authoring a duplicate -- a trained hawk spots trouble before any scout on foot does.

## Her adventure: "The Hawk That Didn't Come Back"
**Objective:** Play as Artemisia tracking and recovering a lost trained hawk using calls, patience, and reading its likely behavior.

**Her skill:** Directly playable patience-and-observation beats, distinctly non-combat.

**Your support:** The protagonist covers ground she can't while she calls and watches from a fixed point.

**Payoff:** The hawk's return to her arm, and her rare, visible relief.
