# Dinah Okonkwo

**Status:** romance-eligible companion (`design/characters.json`) &mdash; design only

Age 25. A chemist and pharmacist whose remedies are compounded exactly, not guessed at, distinct from Marguerite's midwifery and Augustine's dentistry.

## Personality
precise, deeply skeptical of quack tonics, warm once trust is earned, takes correct dosage as a moral position, not just a technical one.

## How you meet her
A traveling salesman is selling a tonic that's actively harming people who take it, and she needs help stopping the sales before someone's seriously hurt.

**To recruit her:**
- Help her expose a dangerous quack remedy being sold nearby.
- Trust her compounding over a faster folk remedy.
- Dinah accepts the invitation.

## What she needs
**Raises her strain:** A dangerous remedy sold to someone who trusted the seller over the evidence.

**Recovers it:** Being trusted with exact instructions and having them actually followed.

## Her adventure: "The Tonic That Was Poison"
**Objective:** Play as Dinah proving a popular tonic is actively harmful through careful testing and public demonstration rather than accusation alone.

**Her skill:** Directly playable evidence-based investigation beats: test, compare, demonstrate.

**Your support:** The protagonist confronts the salesman with her findings while she handles the actual chemistry.

**Payoff:** The tonic pulled from sale, and her quiet relief that the proof, not just her word, carried the argument.
