# Louisa Okoye

**Status:** romance-eligible companion (`design/characters.json`) &mdash; design only

Age 22. A schoolteacher heading west to a post that fell through before she arrived, now teaching whoever will sit still.

## Personality
patient, stubbornly hopeful, corrects grammar without meaning to, braver than she looks.

## Appearance and dress
Clearly adult, ordinary but gorgeous woman: slim, small-chested with little or no prominent bust, attractive hip/seat silhouette, healthy and active. Charismatic, comfortable in herself and sexually confident. No approved art exists for this draft.

A modest traveling dress kept neat despite the trail, a satchel of books she won't leave behind, ink-stained fingers.

*Art status: unapproved draft.*

## How you meet her
Her promised teaching post at a rail town evaporated when the town itself didn't materialize; she's stranded with a trunk of books and nowhere to use them.

**To recruit her:**
- Help her recover a trunk of schoolbooks lost off a wagon.
- Let her actually teach someone something.
- Louisa accepts the invitation.

## What she needs
**Raises her strain:** Being told educating anyone out here is a waste of her time.

**Recovers it:** Someone actually listening to a lesson.

## Her adventure: "The Lesson Nobody Asked For"
**Objective:** Play as Louisa teaching a skeptical outfit hand to read well enough to write home, one patient session at a time.

**Her skill:** Directly playable teaching beats: patience, small wins, one real breakthrough.

**Your support:** The protagonist volunteers to go first so nobody else has to be embarrassed alone.

**Payoff:** The hand reads his own letter out loud, badly, and everyone lets him finish.
