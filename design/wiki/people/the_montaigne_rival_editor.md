# Silas Cobbett

**Status:** minor/supporting character (`design/npcs.json`), category **antagonist**

Age 48. Editor of a rival Providence broadsheet that prints whatever sells.

**Location:** Providence

## Personality
cynical, genuinely talented, wasted on sensationalism, not evil, just chose money over accuracy long ago.

## Why she/he exists
The specific person behind the exciting-false-story competition in Prisca Montaigne's encounter hook, giving her professional rivalry an actual face.

## Appearance
Adult man, late forties, ink-stained but more polished than Prisca's shop, a hint of genuine talent under the cynicism.

## Voice
> Truth sells fine. Excitement sells better. I stopped pretending otherwise years back.

> Montaigne's paper is more honest than mine. It's also read by about a third as many people.

> I'm not sorry. I am, occasionally, a little embarrassed. Different feeling entirely.
