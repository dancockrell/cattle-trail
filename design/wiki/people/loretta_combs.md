# Loretta Combs

**Status:** romance-eligible companion (`design/characters.json`) &mdash; design only

Age 22. A quilter and textile artist whose work tells the county's stories in fabric better than most written records.

## Personality
observant, quietly perceptive about people, patient with slow, careful work, generous with what she makes.

## Appearance and dress
Clearly adult, ordinary but gorgeous woman: slim, small-chested with little or no prominent bust, attractive hip/seat silhouette, healthy and active. Charismatic, comfortable in herself and sexually confident. No approved art exists for this draft.

A practical dress she likely made herself, a workbasket of fabric scraps organized with clear intention, careful precise hands.

*Art status: unapproved draft.*

## How you meet her
She's collecting fabric and stories for a quilt documenting the county's recent strange happenings, and the outfit's own experiences are exactly the kind of thing she's looking to record.

**To recruit her:**
- Recognize the story in one of her finished pieces.
- Contribute something -- fabric, a story, time -- to a quilt in progress.
- Loretta accepts the invitation.

## What she needs
**Raises her strain:** A story or a piece of fabric lost before it could be recorded.

**Recovers it:** Being given a story to keep rather than being asked to perform one.

## Her adventure: "The Quilt That Remembers"
**Objective:** Play as Loretta piecing together a section of her quilt from fabric and stories gathered along the trail, choosing what belongs together.

**Her skill:** Directly playable pattern-and-narrative-arrangement beats: a genuinely different kind of puzzle than combat or repair.

**Your support:** The protagonist contributes an actual piece of their own story to the work.

**Payoff:** A finished section of the quilt that includes the protagonist's own story, stitched in permanently.
