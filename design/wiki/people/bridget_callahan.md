# Bridget Callahan

**Status:** romance-eligible companion (`design/characters.json`) &mdash; design only

Age 23. A postal rider who covers more of the county alone than most outfits do as a group.

## Personality
independent, knows every trail shortcut and every reason not to use them, terse but not unfriendly, takes the mail's reliability personally.

## Appearance and dress
Clearly adult, ordinary but gorgeous woman: slim, small-chested with little or no prominent bust, attractive hip/seat silhouette, healthy and active. Charismatic, comfortable in herself and sexually confident. No approved art exists for this draft.

Practical, weatherproofed riding gear built for long solo rides in any weather, a locked mail satchel she never lets out of reach.

*Art status: unapproved draft.*

## How you meet her
A washed-out crossing nearly cost her the mail satchel and her horse both, and she needs an escort to finish the route before the delay becomes a real problem for the people waiting on it.

**To recruit her:**
- Help her recover the mail after a genuine mishap -- storm, robbery, injury.
- Never ask her to read someone else's letter, even out of curiosity.
- Bridget accepts the invitation.

## What she needs
**Raises her strain:** The mail failing to arrive and someone's important news arriving too late because of it.

**Recovers it:** Being trusted to finish a job alone, and having someone glad to see her when she does.

## Perk: Between the Footprints
Reuses the existing shared trail-hazard perk definition rather than authoring a duplicate -- nobody reads trail conditions better than the rider who does it daily.

## Her adventure: "The Route That Almost Broke Her"
**Objective:** Play as Bridget completing a genuinely dangerous mail route after a bad accident, prioritizing the satchel's safety over her own comfort.

**Her skill:** Directly playable route-and-hazard-reading beats under real physical strain.

**Your support:** The protagonist rides escort and takes the physical risks she can't afford to take with the mail in hand.

**Payoff:** The mail delivered on time despite everything, and her rare admission that she couldn't have finished alone.
