# Sallie Voss

**Status:** minor/supporting character (`design/npcs.json`), category **family**

Age 19. Nettie Voss's daughter, learning the trading-post trade.

**Location:** Fort Griffin

## Personality
eager to prove herself, softer-edged than her mother, sharp underneath it.

## Why she/he exists
Gives Nettie Voss a family tie and a future succession question -- does the trading post stay Nettie's sharp operation or does Sallie's warmer instinct change it. Not a recruitable companion; a town-continuity character.

## Appearance
Adult woman, nineteen, dressed like her mother but softer about it, quick to smile.
