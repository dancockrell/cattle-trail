## How you meet her

**To recruit her:**
- Trust her judgment about when a situation is actually dangerous versus just looking dangerous.
- Don't mistake her comic performance for a lack of real skill.
- Yasmin accepts the invitation.

## What she needs

**Recovers it:** Being thanked specifically, not just applauded generically as part of a show.
