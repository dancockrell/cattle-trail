# The Lantern Man

**Status:** minor/supporting character (`design/npcs.json`), category **supernatural**

Age not stated. A man who should have stayed buried and did not, per LORE.md's list.

**Location:** unplaced

## Personality
polite in a way that is itself the warning sign, repeats the same small talk each encounter, does not seem to know he's dead.

## Why she/he exists
Distinct from the Ford Widow and the Drover Who Isn't -- this haunting's wrongness is social rather than physical: he offers ordinary trail courtesy (a shared fire, directions, a lantern to borrow) and the lantern he lends is always returned somewhere he never was. Late sign material per LORE.md, not an early one.

## Appearance
An ordinary-looking older traveler by firelight; his wrongness should surface only in small temporal/spatial inconsistencies, not appearance.

## Voice
> Evening. Cold one. You're welcome to share the fire, such as it is.

> Take the lantern. I've got no more need of the light than I do the road.

> I keep meaning to get back to town. Feels like I've been meaning to for some time now.
