# Modesty Kane

**Status:** romance-eligible companion (`design/characters.json`) &mdash; design only

Age 23. A traveling photographer chasing the Weird West with a camera instead of a gun.

## Personality
patient in a way that unnerves people, fascinated by ordinary detail, underestimated because of her name, quietly fearless.

## Appearance and dress
Clearly adult, ordinary but gorgeous woman: slim, small-chested with little or no prominent bust, attractive hip/seat silhouette, healthy and active. Charismatic, comfortable in herself and sexually confident. No approved art exists for this draft.

Practical traveling clothes built to carry equipment, a battered camera case she guards more than her own comfort, ink-stained hands from developing plates.

*Art status: unapproved draft.*

## How you meet her
She's trying to photograph the crossing spirit at the ford, convinced (wrongly, or not) that a plate can hold what a story can't.

**To recruit her:**
- Sit still for a photograph despite yourself.
- Protect her equipment through something dangerous.
- Modesty accepts the invitation.

## What she needs
**Raises her strain:** A photograph developing into something she didn't take.

**Recovers it:** Being the one who gets to look, for once, instead of being looked at.

## Perk: Good Company
Reuses the existing shared camp-company perk definition rather than authoring a duplicate.

## Her adventure: "The Plate That Didn't Develop Right"
**Objective:** Play as Modesty developing a photograph under time pressure before the light and chemicals ruin the plate, uncertain what will actually appear on it.

**Her skill:** Directly playable darkroom timing beats: real tension without a jump scare.

**Your support:** The protagonist keeps watch and keeps the tent dark while she works.

**Payoff:** Whatever the plate shows, she shares it with you first, unedited.
