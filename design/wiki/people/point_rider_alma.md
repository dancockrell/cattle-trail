# Alma Okafor

**Status:** minor/supporting character (`design/npcs.json`), category **outfit_hand**

Age 30. The outfit's point rider, reads the trail ahead before the herd commits to it.

**Location:** Clear Fork

## Personality
watchful, economical with words, trusted by Pardo, which is not easily earned.

## Why she/he exists
A third piece of the outfit's own background crew, distinct from Pardo (institutional memory) and Dutch (green enthusiasm) -- she's the one whose judgment the trail actually depends on day to day.

## Appearance
Adult woman, thirty, lean and alert, dressed for long hours in the saddle rather than for looks.

## Voice
> Ground's soft past that ridge. Take the herd around, not through.

> I ride ahead so nobody's surprised. Surprised is how outfits lose stock.

> Pardo trusts my eyes. That took two years. Yours is still open for negotiation.
