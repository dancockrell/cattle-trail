# County Land Office Clerk Purdy

**Status:** minor/supporting character (`design/npcs.json`), category **town**

Age 39. The clerk who commissioned both Beatrix Lowe's and Matilda Greaves's conflicting surveys.

**Location:** unplaced

## Personality
overworked, genuinely just wants the paperwork to match reality, caught in the middle without meaning to be.

## Why she/he exists
Explains, mundanely, why two different cartographers ended up mapping the same disputed stretch -- an overworked clerk who commissioned both without realizing they'd conflict, rather than any deliberate scheme.

## Appearance
Adult man, late thirties, harried office clerk, stacks of unfiled land records visible behind him.

## Voice
> I didn't know they'd disagree. I just wanted the county's records updated before the spring filings.

> Miss Lowe charges more and takes longer. Miss Greaves is faster and cheaper. I hedged. Apparently that was a mistake.

> Bring me one map both of them sign, and I'll never commission a second opinion again as long as I live.
