# Araceli Pena

**Status:** romance-eligible companion (`design/characters.json`) &mdash; design only

Age 24. A horse doctor whose skill with animals is trusted further than most human doctors' skill with people.

## Personality
calm around anything with hooves or teeth, impatient with cruelty, dry sense of humor, fiercely protective of her patients.

## Appearance and dress
Clearly adult, ordinary but gorgeous woman: slim, small-chested with little or no prominent bust, attractive hip/seat silhouette, healthy and active. Charismatic, comfortable in herself and sexually confident. No approved art exists for this draft.

Practical riding clothes built to work close to a nervous animal, a satchel of veterinary tools, calm and unhurried movement.

*Art status: unapproved draft.*

## How you meet her
A horse the outfit needs is going lame and the usual remedies aren't working; she's the only one within reach who actually knows why.

**To recruit her:**
- Trust her diagnosis over a faster, worse fix for a sick animal.
- Don't rush her around an animal in pain.
- Araceli accepts the invitation.

## What she needs
**Raises her strain:** An animal suffering because someone wouldn't wait for the right fix.

**Recovers it:** Sitting with an animal through a hard recovery.

## Perk: Trail Hand
Reuses the existing rancher-family perk definition rather than authoring a duplicate.

## Her adventure: "The Horse Nobody Could Read"
**Objective:** Play as Araceli diagnosing and treating a horse's real injury after everyone else guessed wrong.

**Her skill:** Directly playable diagnostic beats: read gait, temperament and injury correctly before treating.

**Your support:** The protagonist keeps the horse calm while she works.

**Payoff:** The horse finally puts weight on the leg again, and she lets herself be pleased about it in front of you.
