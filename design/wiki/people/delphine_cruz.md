# Delphine Cruz

**Status:** romance-eligible companion (`design/characters.json`) &mdash; design only

Age 24. A card sharp who left a riverboat circuit one step ahead of a reputation she'd earned honestly.

## Personality
cool-headed, sly humor, generous once she trusts you, reads people for a living.

## Appearance and dress
Clearly adult, ordinary but gorgeous woman: slim, small-chested with little or no prominent bust, attractive hip/seat silhouette, healthy and active. Charismatic, comfortable in herself and sexually confident. No approved art exists for this draft.

A fitted vest over a tucked blouse, sleeves gartered for cards, a skirt cut for sitting all night at a table, a hidden card sleeve she'll show you if she likes you.

*Art status: unapproved draft.*

## How you meet her
She's running a game out of the back of a Fort Griffin saloon when the outfit's luck (or lack of it) catches her attention.

**To recruit her:**
- Beat her fairly at a hand of cards, or catch her cheating and say nothing.
- Offer her a reason to stay besides the next game.
- Delphine accepts the invitation.

## What she needs
**Raises her strain:** Being cheated by someone she trusted.

**Recovers it:** A fair hand and an honest answer, in that order.

## Perk: Good Company
Reuses the existing shared camp-company perk definition rather than authoring a duplicate.

## Her adventure: "The Hand She Didn't Play"
**Objective:** Play as Delphine talking a nervous rancher out of betting his own herd on a bad hand, without taking his money herself.

**Her skill:** Directly playable reading of tells and a persuasion beat that doesn't require a fight.

**Your support:** The protagonist backs her read without knowing the cards themselves.

**Payoff:** A private laugh afterward about how close he came to losing everything over a pair of eights.
