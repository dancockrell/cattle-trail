# Brother Amos

**Status:** minor/supporting character (`design/npcs.json`), category **wanderer**

Age 52. An itinerant preacher circuit-riding between Providence, Griffin and smaller camps.

**Location:** unplaced

## Personality
gentle, genuinely humble, distinct from Reverend Pike -- has no fixed church at all.

## Why she/he exists
A wandering counterpart to Reverend Pike's settled congregation, useful for delivering a rumor or a moment of grace somewhere with no church building at all.

## Appearance
Adult man, early fifties, simple worn coat, saddlebags that carry more sermons than possessions.

## Voice
> I preach wherever the fire is lit and someone's willing to listen. Most nights that's enough of a church.

> Pike's got a roof going up. I've got a horse and a Bible. We compare notes when our trails cross.

> I've buried more strangers than I've married couples out here. I try not to let that harden me.
