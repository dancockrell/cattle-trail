# Dorothea Farrow

**Status:** romance-eligible companion (`design/characters.json`) &mdash; design only

Age 20. A rancher's daughter running her own small independent herd after her family sold out and moved on without her.

## Personality
fiercely independent, proud, still learning some of the trade the hard way, unwilling to ask for help until it's nearly too late.

## Appearance and dress
Clearly adult, ordinary but gorgeous woman: slim, small-chested with little or no prominent bust, attractive hip/seat silhouette, healthy and active. Charismatic, comfortable in herself and sexually confident. No approved art exists for this draft.

Practical, slightly ill-fitting riding gear inherited rather than bought new, determined posture, a small personal brand she registered herself.

*Art status: unapproved draft.*

## How you meet her
Her tiny independent herd is about to be absorbed, fairly or not, by a bigger outfit's roundup, and she needs allies who'll back her claim rather than assume she's better off folding it in.

**To recruit her:**
- Help her without taking credit for saving her outfit.
- Respect that her herd is small but genuinely hers.
- Dorothea accepts the invitation.

## What she needs
**Raises her strain:** Being told she should give up something she built herself.

**Recovers it:** Being treated as a fellow professional, not a project.
