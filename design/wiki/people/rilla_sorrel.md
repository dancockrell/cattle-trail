# Rilla Sorrel

**Status:** romance-eligible companion (`design/characters.json`) &mdash; design only

Age 22. Learned gunsmithing from a father who trusted her hands more than his own after they started shaking; now she keeps a bench at Fort Griffin fixing and fitting the rifles and revolvers every outfit's survival actually depends on, and charges honestly enough that soldiers and outfits both come back.

## Personality
precise, plainspoken, unimpressed by bravado, takes quiet pride in a clean mechanism.

## Appearance and dress
Clearly adult, ordinary but gorgeous woman: slim, small-chested with little or no prominent bust, attractive hip/seat silhouette, healthy and active. Charismatic, comfortable in herself and sexually confident. No approved art exists for this draft.

A leather work apron over a plain shirt with cuffs faintly stained by gun oil, a loupe on a cord around her neck, hair pinned back out of the workbench's way.

*Art status: unapproved draft.*

## How you meet her
The outfit's rifle jams at the worst moment on a Fort Griffin supply run, and her bench is the only one in town with a line short enough to fix it before dark.

**To recruit her:**
- Bring her the rifle and let her actually diagnose it instead of describing the problem for her.
- Pay her fair asking price without haggling her down out of habit.
- Rilla accepts the invitation.

## What she needs
**Raises her strain:** A weapon that fails someone because it was never fitted right in the first place.

**Recovers it:** Sitting with her at the bench while she works something apart and back together, no talking required.

## Perk: Sure-Handed Tuning
Reuses the existing repair-efficiency perk definition; a rifle stripped and reset by her hands runs true longer than one merely cleaned.

## Her adventure: "The Rifle That Jammed"
**Objective:** Play as Rilla stripping and refitting a failing rifle mechanism against a real clock before the outfit rides out at first light.

**Her skill:** Directly playable precise bench work and part-by-part diagnosis under time pressure, not a menu.

**Your support:** The protagonist holds the lamp steady and hands her the right tool when she calls for it, without being asked twice.

**Payoff:** She test-fires it once into a sandbag, nods once, and lets the protagonist see her actually pleased with her own work.
