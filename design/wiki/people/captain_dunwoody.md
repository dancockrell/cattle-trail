# Captain Dunwoody

**Status:** minor/supporting character (`design/npcs.json`), category **town**

Age 48. Commanding officer of the Fort Griffin garrison.

**Location:** Fort Griffin

## Personality
formally correct, privately weary of frontier postings, answers to distant orders that rarely fit local reality.

## Why she/he exists
Gives Corporal Ness's garrison a commanding officer and a source of official (as opposed to gossip) information, distinct from Sheriff Dell's civilian authority.

## Appearance
Adult man, late forties, uniform kept correct even in frontier heat, controlled bearing.

## Voice
> My orders come from further away than this county understands.

> I keep this garrison out of local disputes. It's the only policy that's worked so far.

> Dell and I have an understanding. Neither of us wants the other's job.
