# Cleo Bannister

**Status:** romance-eligible companion (`design/characters.json`) &mdash; design only

Age 23. A barber whose shop is the county's most reliable source of both a clean shave and reliable gossip.

## Personality
chatty, genuinely skilled with a razor, reads people while her hands are busy, warm to regulars, professional to strangers.

## Appearance and dress
Clearly adult, ordinary but gorgeous woman: slim, small-chested with little or no prominent bust, attractive hip/seat silhouette, healthy and active. Charismatic, comfortable in herself and sexually confident. No approved art exists for this draft.

A practical barber's apron over a neat dress, a leather roll of razors and shears she maintains meticulously.

*Art status: unapproved draft.*

## How you meet her
She's the only barber for two counties and offers the outfit a proper shave and haircut along with the most useful gossip they've heard all week, free of charge as an opener.

**To recruit her:**
- Trust her with a straight razor and don't flinch.
- Actually listen to what she's heard while she works.
- Cleo accepts the invitation.

## What she needs
**Raises her strain:** A customer flinching or distrusting her skill mid-shave.

**Recovers it:** Conversation that flows easily without needing to be forced.
