# Constance Whitlock

**Status:** romance-eligible companion (`design/characters.json`) &mdash; design only

Age 27. A bookkeeper and accountant whose ledgers have quietly saved more outfits than anyone credits her for.

## Personality
precise, dry wit, unshockable, protective of the people whose numbers she keeps.

## Appearance and dress
Clearly adult, ordinary but gorgeous woman: slim, small-chested with little or no prominent bust, attractive hip/seat silhouette, healthy and active. Charismatic, comfortable in herself and sexually confident. No approved art exists for this draft.

Neat, practical clothing suited to close paperwork, spectacles she takes off to make a point, ink-stained cuffs.

*Art status: unapproved draft.*

## How you meet her
She catches a discrepancy in a trade deal that would have cost the outfit dearly, uninvited and unpaid for the favor.

**To recruit her:**
- Let her find the error in the outfit's own books.
- Take her correction without getting defensive.
- Constance accepts the invitation.

## What she needs
**Raises her strain:** Numbers that don't add up and nobody else noticing.

**Recovers it:** Being asked her opinion before a deal, not after it fails.

## Her adventure: "The Ledger That Lied"
**Objective:** Play as Constance auditing a deliberately falsified set of trade books to find exactly where and how they were altered.

**Her skill:** Directly playable close-reading and cross-referencing, not a lecture about numbers.

**Your support:** The protagonist runs interference with the person whose books are being checked.

**Payoff:** She finds the error, states it plainly, and doesn't gloat about being right.
