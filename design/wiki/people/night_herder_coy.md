# Coy Ashworth

**Status:** minor/supporting character (`design/npcs.json`), category **outfit_hand**

Age 34. Rides night guard on the herd.

**Location:** Clear Fork

## Personality
superstitious in a practical way, sings low to calm cattle, doesn't scare easy but keeps a tally of what does, loyal.

## Why she/he exists
Rides night guard on the herd and is usually first to notice a spook with a cause the daylight crew won't credit. Treats a bad night the way Ines treats a bad track -- as information, not omen.

## Appearance
Adult man in his early thirties, quiet watchfulness, a low singing voice he uses more than his talking one.

## Voice
> They went up all together, same second. Cattle don't do that for nothing.

> I'll sing to them till sunup if it keeps every head where I can count it.

> Something walked the far side of that herd tonight. Wasn't one of us.
