# Marcella Iturbe

**Status:** romance-eligible companion (`design/characters.json`) &mdash; design only

Age 26. A wainwright who builds and rebuilds wagons rather than just patching them, distinct from Naomi Freeman's general blacksmith trade.

## Personality
methodical, takes structural integrity personally, unimpressed by decoration over function, warm once a shared project is underway.

## How you meet her
The outfit's wagon frame is cracked in a way that will fail catastrophically later if it's only patched now, and she's the only wainwright who'll tell them that plainly instead of taking the easy job.

**To recruit her:**
- Bring her a genuinely broken wagon worth rebuilding rather than replacing.
- Trust her structural judgment over a faster patch job.
- Marcella accepts the invitation.

## What she needs
**Raises her strain:** A structural failure that could have been prevented by someone honest about the real problem.

**Recovers it:** Working a long, physical job alongside someone rather than being managed.

## Her adventure: "The Frame That Had to Be Rebuilt"
**Objective:** Play as Marcella rebuilding a wagon frame properly under time pressure, refusing a faster patch that would fail later.

**Her skill:** Directly playable structural-repair beats: measure, cut, fit, test -- no shortcuts rewarded.

**Your support:** The protagonist holds joints steady and hands over tools in the right order, learning as they go.

**Payoff:** A wagon that will actually survive the rest of the trail, and her quiet, earned confidence in it.
