# Hepzibah Thorne

**Status:** romance-eligible companion (`design/characters.json`) &mdash; design only

Age 26. A clockmaker and watch repairer whose precise trade rarely gets appreciated on a trail where nobody's in a hurry to know the exact time.

## Personality
exacting, finds calm in small precise mechanisms, dryly funny about being asked to fix everything mechanical regardless of size, patient.

## Appearance and dress
Clearly adult, ordinary but gorgeous woman: slim, small-chested with little or no prominent bust, attractive hip/seat silhouette, healthy and active. Charismatic, comfortable in herself and sexually confident. No approved art exists for this draft.

Neat practical clothes suited to close, careful work, a jeweler's loupe on a cord, tools small enough to fit in a single case.

## How you meet her
The outfit's one reliable pocket watch, used for coordinating everything from cattle counts to camp rotations, breaks at the worst possible time, and she's the only one who can fix something that small.

**To recruit her:**
- Bring her something genuinely small and precise to repair, not a wagon axle.
- Don't confuse her trade with Ada's or Naomi's larger mechanical work.
- Hepzibah accepts the invitation.

## What she needs
**Raises her strain:** Being asked to fix something clumsy and large as if fine mechanism skill scales up freely.

**Recovers it:** Quiet, steady-handed company during close work.

## Her adventure: "The Watch That Kept Everyone Honest"
**Objective:** Play as Hepzibah repairing a precise, tiny mechanism under magnification, genuinely delicate work rather than a repair minigame reused from Ada's or Naomi's larger-scale fixes.

**Her skill:** Directly playable fine-motor precision beats distinct in feel from the game's other repair adventures.

**Your support:** The protagonist holds the light steady and hands over tools sized for the job.

**Payoff:** The watch running true again, and her quiet pride in work nobody else notices until it's broken.
