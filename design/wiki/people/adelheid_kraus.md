precise about her craft, guarded about her recipe, warms slowly but genuinely, unbothered by the trade's rough reputation.

## How you meet her
Questions about the legality and taxation of her small operation threaten to shut her down just as a batch reaches its best, and she needs help navigating the situation without losing everything.

**To recruit her:**
- Help her protect her still from revenue concerns or a jealous rival.
- Actually appreciate her craft rather than just the product.
- Adelheid accepts the invitation.

## What she needs
**Raises her strain:** A carefully made batch ruined by impatience or bureaucratic interference.

**Recovers it:** Someone appreciating the actual craft, not just what it produces.

**Objective:** Play as Adelheid navigating a legal/bureaucratic threat to her operation while protecting a time-sensitive batch.
