# Maren "Cookie" Holt

**Status:** minor/supporting character (`design/npcs.json`), category **outfit_hand**

Age 41. The outfit's chuckwagon cook, runs the wagon and the schedule as much as the food.

**Location:** Clear Fork

## Personality
blunt, keeps count of everything, protective of the hands under her, no patience for wasted food.

## Why she/he exists
Feeds the outfit and keeps its unofficial ledger of who's owed what favor. A quieter authority than Pardo's -- disputes get settled over her fire before they ever reach the trail boss.

## Appearance
Adult woman in her early forties, sturdy build, sleeves rolled past the elbow, permanent flour dust on her forearms.

## Voice
> Eat it now or carry it hungry. Your choice.

> I've fed worse-tempered outfits than this one. Barely.

> A man who won't eat won't ride straight. Sit down.
