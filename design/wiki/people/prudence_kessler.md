## How you meet her
A burial goes wrong in a small, specific way (a grave collapse, a delayed arrival, a dispute over the deceased's wishes) and she needs practical help to see it through with the dignity the family deserves.

**To recruit her:**
- Help her manage an unusually difficult burial with dignity.
- Don't flinch or make her trade a joke to her face.
- Prudence accepts the invitation.

## What she needs
**Raises her strain:** A family denied a dignified goodbye because of circumstance or someone else's carelessness.

**Recovers it:** Being treated as an ordinary tradeswoman, not a symbol of death itself.
