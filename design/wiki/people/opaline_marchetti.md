## How you meet her
Someone is passing off cheap plated goods as her genuine silverwork, damaging her reputation, and she needs help catching the forger before it costs her the trade entirely.

**To recruit her:**
- Bring her a piece worth the trouble of real silverwork.
- Notice the difference between her work and mass-made goods.
- Opaline accepts the invitation.

## What she needs
**Raises her strain:** Her name used to sell something she'd never have made.

**Recovers it:** Being trusted to identify real from fake without having to argue the point.

## Her adventure: "The Silver That Wasn't Hers"
**Objective:** Play as Opaline identifying and proving counterfeit goods sold under her name through careful metallurgical examination.

**Her skill:** Directly playable close-examination beats: weight, hallmark, finish -- the tells a forger misses.

**Your support:** The protagonist confronts the seller while she demonstrates the proof.

**Payoff:** Her name cleared publicly, and a small, genuine piece made specifically for the protagonist as thanks.
