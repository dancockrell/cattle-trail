# Otis Beckwith

**Status:** minor/supporting character (`design/npcs.json`), category **town**

Age 39. Fort Griffin's telegraph office manager, Prudence Hale's superior.

**Location:** Fort Griffin

## Personality
by-the-book, not unkind, just tired, protective of his one skilled operator.

## Why she/he exists
Ties Prudence Hale's companion record to a real supervisor and workplace, and gives the telegraph office a second voice besides hers.

## Appearance
Adult man, late thirties, ink-stained shirt cuffs, permanently harried expression.

## Voice

> I don't read the private wires. I just notice which ones come marked urgent too often.
