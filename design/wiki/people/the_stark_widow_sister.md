# Eulalie Stark

**Status:** minor/supporting character (`design/npcs.json`), category **town**

Age 59. Gideon's sister, the other Bellhollow holdout.

**Location:** Bellhollow

## Personality
more practical than Gideon, keeps the camp's one lit window lit, worried but loyal.

## Why she/he exists
The reason Bellhollow isn't fully abandoned: she stays because Gideon won't leave, not because she isn't afraid. A more sympathetic entry point into the location than Gideon's stubbornness alone.

## Appearance
Adult woman, late fifties, practical widow's dress, tired but not broken.

## Voice
> He won't leave the claim. I won't leave him. That's the whole arrangement.

> I keep that window lit so he can find his way back from the workings. Some nights that's the only reason he does.

> Ask him about the bell and you'll get a riddle. Ask me and I'll tell you I don't know either, plainly.
