# Briony Wexford

**Status:** romance-eligible companion (`design/characters.json`) &mdash; design only

Age 24. A cheese-maker and dairy farmer whose small operation produces the county's only reliably good cheese.

## Personality
patient with slow processes, protective of her herd's welfare, warm and unhurried, quietly proud of a craft most people take for granted.

## Appearance and dress
Clearly adult, ordinary but gorgeous woman: slim, small-chested with little or no prominent bust, attractive hip/seat silhouette, healthy and active. Charismatic, comfortable in herself and sexually confident. No approved art exists for this draft.

Practical dairy-work clothes, an apron marked by the trade, calm unhurried manner suited to slow careful processes.

*Art status: unapproved draft.*

## How you meet her
Part of her small dairy herd is sick or threatened and her entire season's production depends on catching the problem before it spreads to the rest.

**To recruit her:**
- Help her protect her dairy herd from a real threat -- rustlers, disease, or worse.
- Actually appreciate her cheese rather than treating it as an afterthought.
- Briony accepts the invitation.

## What she needs
**Raises her strain:** A herd lost to a problem that early attention could have caught.

**Recovers it:** Slow, patient company during a long process, not rushed visits.

## Perk: Trail Hand
Reuses the existing rancher-family perk definition rather than authoring a duplicate -- herd care crosses over from cattle to dairy stock cleanly.

## Her adventure: "The Herd That Went Off"
**Objective:** Play as Briony diagnosing and treating a threat to her dairy herd before it spreads, balancing care with practicality.

**Her skill:** Directly playable animal-care triage beats distinct in tone from Araceli's single-patient veterinary adventure.

**Your support:** The protagonist helps isolate and calm the affected animals while she treats them.

**Payoff:** A herd saved and a season's cheese secured, shared generously rather than sold at the first opportunity.
