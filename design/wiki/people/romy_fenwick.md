# Romy Fenwick

**Status:** romance-eligible companion (`design/characters.json`) &mdash; design only

Age 22. A miller running the county's one reliable grist mill, keeping half the settlements fed.

## Personality
steady, unbothered by the mill's constant noise and grind, protective of the mill's water rights, warm and practical.

## Appearance and dress
Clearly adult, ordinary but gorgeous woman: slim, small-chested with little or no prominent bust, attractive hip/seat silhouette, healthy and active. Charismatic, comfortable in herself and sexually confident. No approved art exists for this draft.

Practical work clothes dusted permanently with flour, strong capable hands, a habit of checking the millstream without noticing she's doing it.

## How you meet her
Someone upstream is diverting water in a way that threatens to stop her mill entirely, and the settlements depending on her flour need her help resolving it before the grain runs out.

**To recruit her:**
- Help her defend the mill's water rights from an upstream diversion.
- Trust her mechanical read of the millworks.
- Romy accepts the invitation.

## What she needs
**Raises her strain:** A whole settlement going hungry because of one person's water diversion upstream.

**Recovers it:** Practical, working company at the mill rather than being visited like an attraction.

**Objective:** Play as Romy resolving an upstream water diversion through negotiation and mechanical adjustment rather than force.

**Her skill:** Directly playable resource-management beats: flow, pressure, and a negotiated compromise.

**Your support:** The protagonist represents her interests to the upstream party while she manages the mill's mechanism.

**Payoff:** The wheel turning again, and flour enough for the county, delivered with her practical pride rather than fanfare.
