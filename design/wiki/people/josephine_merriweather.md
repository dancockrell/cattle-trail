## How you meet her
The river trade has dried up for the season and she's looking for steam work on land, arriving right as the outfit's own machinery trouble makes her timing convenient.

**To recruit her:**
- Trust her steam expertise even though it's riverboat, not walker, experience.
- Help her find honest work while the river trade is slow.
- Josephine accepts the invitation.

## What she needs
**Raises her strain:** A boiler mismanaged by someone who didn't respect what it could do.

**Recovers it:** Talking shop with someone who actually understands the machinery.

## Her adventure: "The Landlocked Engineer"
**Objective:** Play as Josephine adapting riverboat steam expertise to fix a walker or cart engine she's never worked on before, reasoning from first principles.

**Her skill:** Directly playable cross-domain problem solving: the same physics, an unfamiliar machine.

**Your support:** The protagonist describes the machine's usual behavior while she works it out.

**Payoff:** The machine runs true, and she admits, grudgingly, that land engineering has its own appeal.
