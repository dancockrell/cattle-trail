# Electra Finsbury

**Status:** romance-eligible companion (`design/characters.json`) &mdash; design only

Age 24. A watchmaker's rival specialty: an electrician experimenting with early battery and wire work most of the county has never seen.

## Personality
fascinated by invisible forces, careful about real danger she understands better than anyone nearby, patient teacher when asked, quietly thrilled by her own field.

## Appearance and dress
Clearly adult, ordinary but gorgeous woman: slim, small-chested with little or no prominent bust, attractive hip/seat silhouette, healthy and active. Charismatic, comfortable in herself and sexually confident. No approved art exists for this draft.

Practical work clothes with rubber-soled boots for safety, wire and battery components in a fitted case, careful deliberate movements.

*Art status: unapproved draft.*

## How you meet her
A test of hers goes wrong near the trail, damaging her equipment and nearly hurting her, and she needs practical help recovering it safely without anyone getting shocked in the process.

**To recruit her:**
- Trust her judgment about something that looks harmless but isn't.
- Help her recover equipment lost in a bad experiment.
- Electra accepts the invitation.

## What she needs
**Raises her strain:** Being dismissed as reckless for a field nobody else understands well enough to judge fairly.

**Recovers it:** Someone actually listening when she explains a real danger instead of laughing it off.

## Perk: Sure-Handed Tuning

## Her adventure: "The Current That Almost Caught Her"
**Objective:** Play as Electra safely recovering damaged electrical equipment, respecting real hazards the protagonist has never had to consider before.

**Her skill:** Directly playable hazard-aware careful-handling beats, introducing a genuinely new kind of danger to the game's repair-adventure family.

**Your support:** The protagonist follows her exact safety instructions without improvising.

**Payoff:** Equipment recovered safely, and her rare, delighted explanation of what she was actually trying to build.
