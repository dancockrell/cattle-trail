# Del Ochoa

**Status:** minor/supporting character (`design/npcs.json`), category **outfit_hand**

Age 39. The outfit's farrier, keeps horses shod and tack and hardware from failing on the trail.

**Location:** Clear Fork

## Personality
methodical, takes pride in a clean repair, gets along fine with Ada Mercer's steam contraptions, dry sense of humor.

## Why she/he exists
Keeps the outfit's horses shod and its leather and hardware from failing halfway down the trail. The natural hand to loop in when Ada's steam work needs an ordinary mechanical fix rather than a clever one.

## Appearance
Adult man in his late thirties, forearms thick from anvil work, leather apron scarred by old sparks.

## Voice
> Shoe's been loose two days. You just noticed. I noticed.

> Ada's brass and my anvil solve the same problem most weeks.

> Bring it to me before it breaks, not after. Same rate either way, worse mood.

- Not implemented in scripts/. Gives the outfit an ordinary-mechanical counterpart to Ada Mercer's steam specialty, per LORE.md's rule that machines break in public and have jobs.
